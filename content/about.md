# About this wiki

## Purpose

The South Carolina Elections 2026 Wiki is an independent, source-tracked research project covering statewide elections and the offices on the ballot for Pendleton, South Carolina, in Anderson County.

It is designed to help readers answer practical questions about:

- Who is running for each office
- What the official ballot and candidate status records say
- Which districts and offices cover Pendleton
- What candidates say about their platforms
- How current campaign finance and legislative records can be checked
- Which claims remain uncertain or disputed

This is **not** an official election website, campaign site, news organization, or government record. Official election, ballot, results, and campaign-finance records should be checked through the linked government sources.

## Source hierarchy

The wiki prioritizes evidence in this order:

1. Official election agencies, government offices, legislative records, and campaign filings
2. Official candidate and campaign statements, clearly attributed as campaign claims
3. Reputable journalism and local reporting
4. Secondary databases, historical indexes, and election analysis

Secondary sources are useful for discovery and context, but they do not override official candidate status, ballot status, certified results, or filing records.

## Reading uncertainty markers

- `[disputed]` means that credible sources currently disagree or that an official record has not yet resolved a conflict.
- `[unverified]` means that a claim has not been confirmed by a sufficiently reliable source.
- `[needs-source]` means that the page identifies a factual gap that still needs a citation.
- `confidence: high`, `medium`, or `low` describes the page's overall evidence quality, not the character or quality of a candidate.

Incomplete local candidate fields are left incomplete rather than filled with guesses.

## Finance and influence data

Current-cycle campaign finance is kept separate from career totals and historical context. Federal races use the Federal Election Commission first. South Carolina state and local races use the South Carolina Ethics Commission and relevant local records.

Employer-classified individual donations, corporate PAC contributions, committee transfers, independent expenditures, lobbying data, and derived influence scores are different categories. They should not be combined without preserving the source, denominator, reporting period, and methodology.

## Freshness and corrections

Election status, candidate fields, vote totals, and finance figures can change quickly. Pages include a `last_updated` date and should be read as a dated snapshot. A later official filing, court order, certification, recount, or correction may supersede an earlier page.

The project favors transparent corrections: preserve a material conflict with dates and citations until the evidence supports a resolution.

## Public scope

The public site contains reviewed Markdown pages from the research wiki. Private Obsidian configuration, internal instructions, raw research files, temporary drafts, and personal metadata are not part of the public publication.
