# Governor (2026)

Open seat: incumbent Henry McMaster (R) is term-limited (two consecutive terms). Democrats have not won this office since 1998. All major raters call the general election Solid/Likely Republican.

## Race status

- Primary: June 9, 2026
- Runoff: June 23, 2026
- General election: November 3, 2026
- Candidate field: Republican and Democratic tickets plus two third-party tickets listed; verify write-ins and replacements
- Ratings: Cook Solid R, Inside Elections Solid R, Sabato Safe R; DDHQ not found in the current sources

## Republican ticket - nominee

- [[pages/entities/alan-wilson|Alan Wilson]] (Governor) - sitting Attorney General (2011-present); Ballotpedia currently reports that he won the June 23 runoff with 68.4% (211,555 votes) over Lt. Gov. Pamela Evette. The initial wiki draft recorded 68.6% [disputed].
- [[pages/entities/mike-reichenbach|Mike Reichenbach]] (Lt. Gov) - state senator, SD-31 (2022-present), businessman

Primary history: June 9 first round was a 5-way race (Evette led with ~20%, Trump endorsed her May 29; Kimbrell withdrew June 4; Mace and Norman eliminated and endorsed Wilson). Ballotpedia currently reports Wilson's June 23 runoff result as 68.4%; the initial wiki draft recorded 68.6% [disputed]. Trump co-endorsed both finalists.

The SC Votes results page is the authoritative result source. Its linked ENR endpoint returned a 403 during the August 4 source check, so the 68.4% versus 68.6% discrepancy remains explicitly [disputed] rather than being resolved from Ballotpedia alone.

## Democratic ticket - nominee

- [[pages/entities/jermaine-johnson|Jermaine Johnson]] (Governor) - state rep, HD-52; won Dem primary June 9 with 59.7% (221,751 votes) over Billy Webster (29.7%) and William Mullins McLeod Jr. (10.7%). The initial wiki draft recorded 221,951 votes [disputed].
- [[pages/entities/sam-skardon|Sam Skardon]] (Lt. Gov) - running mate; background not yet researched [needs-source]

## Third party

- [[pages/entities/walid-hakim|Walid Hakim]] (Green) - businessman, Marine veteran

## Issues in play (per campaigns)

- Wilson: law and order, child predator prosecutions, election integrity
- Johnson: funding underfunded state programs, jobs for former felons, ending "single party rule"
- Hakim: "100 Year Plan", education reform, land reform
- Addison: "Unlocking Potential, Together" (platform details scarce)
