# Statewide Constitutional Offices (2026)

All six other statewide offices are on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot. Every race leans heavily Republican; incumbents running again in four of six. All candidate links go to entity pages.

## Race status

- Primary: June 9, 2026
- Runoff: June 23, 2026 if needed
- General election: November 3, 2026
- Candidate field: major-party candidates listed for all six offices; verify replacements and write-ins
- Ratings: not available in current wiki sources

## Attorney General (open - Alan Wilson ran for governor)

- [[pages/entities/david-stumbo|David Stumbo]] (R) - 8th Circuit Solicitor; won the June 23 GOP runoff with 55.75% (173,127 votes) over Stephen Goldfinch (44.25%) per the certified ENR record
- [[pages/entities/richard-hricik|Richard Hricik]] (D) - trial attorney, ex-Arthur Andersen accountant

**Where they disagree:** Campaign style (Stumbo runs a traditional "tough on crime" prosecutor campaign with endorsements; Hricik runs a no-money pledge, volunteer-only campaign refusing special-interest donations) and law-enforcement posture (Stumbo emphasizes aggressive prosecution of illegal aliens, murderers, and drug dealers; Hricik emphasizes officer accountability, citing mask-wearing and lack of body cameras) ([votestumbo.com](https://votestumbo.com), [Live 5 News](https://www.live5news.com/), [Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/)).

## Secretary of State

- [[pages/entities/mark-hammond|Mark Hammond]] (R, incumbent since 2003)
- [[pages/entities/jason-belton|Jason Belton]] (D) - labor organizer

**Where they disagree:** Hammond runs on administrative results (efficiency, charity-fraud enforcement that collected over $5.8M in fines, top-5 charitable giving ranking); Belton frames the office around citizen empowerment and labor rights, and wants the office to actively support small-business and nonprofit formation ([sos.sc.gov](https://sos.sc.gov/about-us/secretarys-biography), [jasonbeltonforsc.com](https://jasonbeltonforsc.com)).

## State Treasurer

- [[pages/entities/curtis-loftis|Curtis Loftis]] (R, incumbent since 2011)
- [[pages/entities/vincent-coe|Vincent Coe]] (D) - bank officer, ex-bank examiner

**Where they disagree:** Loftis points to results (over $420M in unclaimed property returned, pension-reform challenges, financial literacy programs); Coe says outdated systems and limited oversight have harmed the treasury and emphasizes modernization, internal controls, and transparency ([treasurer.sc.gov](https://treasurer.sc.gov/), [WSOC](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/political-beat-candidate-guide-south-carolina-state-treasurer/LYIWGCQOARDQFA6A6YPYO2EVT4/)).

## Comptroller General (open - Brian Gaines not running)

- [[pages/entities/mike-burkhold|Mike Burkhold]] (R) - businessman
- [[pages/entities/tiffany-boozer|Tiffany Boozer]] (D) - comptroller's office QA manager, accountant

**Where they disagree:** Burkhold wants the office to be an active taxpayer "watchdog" with an online state checkbook and public spending database (citing SC's books being off by $3.5B); Boozer, a CPA, emphasizes professional accounting leadership, stronger internal controls, performance metrics, and modernizing the transparency portal ([burkhold.net](https://burkhold.net), [boozerforsc.com](https://boozerforsc.com/)).

## Superintendent of Education

- [[pages/entities/ellen-weaver|Ellen Weaver]] (R, incumbent since 2023)
- [[pages/entities/sylvia-wright|Sylvia Wright]] (D) - educator
- Baba Amin Ojuok (United Citizens) - third-party candidate on the official SC Votes general-election record

**Where they disagree (most contested statewide race):** School choice (Weaver supports expanded vouchers/choice and parental empowerment; Wright focuses on funding public schools and Universal Pre-K for all 4-year-olds), and approach (Weaver pushes the science of reading, literacy/math reforms, and reducing red tape; Wright prioritizes the educator pipeline, paid internships for student teachers, and safe learning environments) ([Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/), [The State](https://www.thestate.com/), [ellenweaver.com](https://ellenweaver.com/), [wright2026sc.com](https://www.wright2026sc.com/about)).

## Commissioner of Agriculture (open - Hugh Weathers retiring)

- [[pages/entities/cody-simpson|Cody Simpson]] (R) - fifth-generation farmer, ex-USDA FSA state director; won the June 23 GOP runoff with 62.58% (193,033 votes) over Danny Ford (37.42%) per the certified ENR record
- [[pages/entities/deshawn-blanding|DeShawn Blanding]] (D) - ag co-op president, farmer
- Chris Nelums (United Citizens) - third-party candidate on the official SC Votes general-election record
- Michael Sullens (Libertarian) - third-party candidate on the official SC Votes general-election record

**Where they disagree:** Simpson emphasizes deregulation and market access (updating post-DHEC food-safety regulations, auditing state contracts so local farmers can sell more to government) and his "America First Farmer" framing; Blanding emphasizes a cooperative model, local processing, food hubs, and rural economic development ("Processing, Partnerships, and Protection"). A direct issue-by-issue comparison is limited by the amount of public platform material ([Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/), [codyforsc.com](https://codyforsc.com), [blandingforsc.com](https://blandingforsc.com), [BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/deshawn-blanding)).
