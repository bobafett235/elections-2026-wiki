# SC House District 8 (2026)

Covers most of Pendleton's school district area (Ballotpedia shows HD-8 overlapping 52% of Anderson SD4) plus parts of Anderson County; incumbent lives in Townville, SC. All 124 House seats are up in 2026.

## Race status

- Primary: June 9, 2026
- Runoff: June 23, 2026 if needed
- General election: November 3, 2026
- Candidate field: Don Chapman and Demetrius Carson listed in the secondary index; verify current ballot status and any write-ins against SC Votes
