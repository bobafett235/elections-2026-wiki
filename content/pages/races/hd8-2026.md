# SC House District 8 (2026)

Covers most of Pendleton's school district area (Ballotpedia shows HD-8 overlapping 52% of Anderson SD4) plus parts of Anderson County; incumbent lives in Townville, SC. All 124 House seats are up in 2026.

## Race status

- Primary: June 9, 2026
- Runoff: June 23, 2026 (Chapman beat Hodges)
- General election: November 3, 2026
- Candidate field: Don Chapman (R, incumbent) and Demetrius Carson (D) confirmed for the general per the Independent Mail runoff report; no write-ins confirmed yet
- Ratings: not available in current wiki sources

## Candidates (Nov 3 general)

- [[pages/entities/don-chapman|Don Chapman]] (R, incumbent) - architect; first elected 2022; won the June 23 GOP runoff over Sherry Hodges (Patrick Orr eliminated June 9) per the [Independent Mail](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/23/sc-election-runoff-results-hodges-vs-chapman-anderson-county-district-8/90648743007/)
- [[pages/entities/demetrius-carson|Demetrius Carson]] (D) - advanced without a primary (canceled); little public info
