# U.S. House, South Carolina districts 1-7 (2026)

South Carolina will elect one U.S. representative from each of its seven congressional districts on November 3, 2026. The primary was June 9 and the primary runoff was June 23. Ballotpedia notes that its candidate lists may be incomplete.

## Race status

- Primary: June 9, 2026
- Runoff: June 23, 2026 if needed
- General election: November 3, 2026
