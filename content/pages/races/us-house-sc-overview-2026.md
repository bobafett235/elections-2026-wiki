# U.S. House, South Carolina districts 1-7 (2026)

South Carolina will elect one U.S. representative from each of its seven congressional districts on November 3, 2026. The primary was June 9 and the primary runoff was June 23. Ballotpedia notes that its candidate lists may be incomplete.

## Race status

- Primary: June 9, 2026
- Runoff: June 23, 2026 if needed
- General election: November 3, 2026
- Candidate field: at-a-glance incumbency map; detailed candidate fields remain incomplete
- Ratings: not available in the current overview sources

## Verification notes

- The Ballotpedia overview confirms seven seats, the June 9 primary, the June 23 runoff, and the November 3 general election.
- The current House overview source identifies the incumbents above, but it warns that candidate lists may be incomplete.
- SC Votes explains that candidates may appear on a general-election ballot for more than one office. Norman's Senate runoff therefore does not itself establish that SC-5 is vacant or that he cannot appear in the House field.
- Districts 5 and 7 still need a direct South Carolina filing or sample-ballot check for complete House candidate fields, but the prior claimed conflict with the Senate page has been removed.
- This page is intentionally an at-a-glance map, not a substitute for a verified candidate page for every district.
