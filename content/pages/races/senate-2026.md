# U.S. Senate (2026) - Special GOP Runoff Aug 25

**Current status (updated Aug 11):** The Aug. 11 special Republican primary did not produce a majority winner. [[pages/entities/darline-graham|Darline Graham]] and [[pages/entities/ralph-norman|Ralph Norman]] advanced to the **Aug. 25 runoff**; [[pages/entities/russell-fry|Russell Fry]] did not advance. The Associated Press called the runoff pair. The official SC Votes results page links to the canonical ENR record, but that CloudFront-hosted record returned 403 from this research environment, so this page does **not** report unofficial vote totals as final.

**Ballot correction:** Twelve people initially filed. The South Carolina Republican Party decertified Danny Ford II, Darius Mitchell, and Clark Neilson on July 28, leaving nine candidates. A Richland County court order required Ford to be added back on Aug. 4, and the State Election Commission confirmed the addition. The Aug. 11 ballot therefore had **10 candidates**. The remaining two decertifications, Mitchell and Neilson, are documented below for filing history but were not ballot candidates.

**The story:** Lindsey Graham won renomination June 9 (56.8% over Mark Lynch's 28.9%), then died July 11, 2026. Gov. McMaster appointed his sister [[pages/entities/darline-graham|Darline Graham]] on July 13 to finish the term. Because the party's nominee died, a special GOP primary was held August 11, followed by an August 25 runoff after no candidate cleared 50%.

**Context:** Only two people have been elected to this seat since 1956 (Strom Thurmond, Lindsey Graham). Trump carried SC with 58% in 2024. Republicans have won every SC Senate race since 2004.

**Ratings (general):** Cook Solid R | DDHQ Likely R | Inside Elections Likely R | Sabato Safe R.

## Special Republican primary - August 11 (10 ballot candidates)

1. [[pages/entities/darline-graham|Darline Graham]] - appointed incumbent; advanced to Aug. 25 runoff
2. [[pages/entities/ralph-norman|Ralph Norman]] - U.S. Rep, SC-5; advanced to Aug. 25 runoff
3. [[pages/entities/russell-fry|Russell Fry]] - U.S. Rep, SC-7; did not advance
4. [[pages/entities/mark-sanford|Mark Sanford]] - former governor (2003-2011), former U.S. Rep
5. [[pages/entities/mark-lynch|Mark Lynch]] - businessman; 28.9% vs Graham in June primary
6. [[pages/entities/mark-mcbride|Mark McBride]] - former Myrtle Beach mayor
7. [[pages/entities/duke-buckner|Duke Buckner]] - attorney; ex-Walterboro councilman
8. [[pages/entities/danny-ford|Danny Ford II]] - reinstated to the ballot by court order; lost Ag Commissioner runoff June 23
9. [[pages/entities/glenda-gail-parker|Glenda Gail Parker]] - former Air Force financial manager
10. [[pages/entities/sam-shepherd|Sam Shepherd]] - first-time candidate; little public info

Darius Mitchell and Clark Neilson were among the original 12 filers but were decertified and did not appear on the Aug. 11 ballot. Ford's initial decertification and court-ordered reinstatement are recorded above.
