# U.S. Senate (2026) - Special GOP Primary Aug 11

**The story:** Lindsey Graham won renomination June 9 (56.8% over Mark Lynch's 28.9%), then died July 11, 2026. Gov. McMaster appointed his sister [[pages/entities/darline-graham|Darline Graham]] on July 13 to finish the term (first sibling appointed to a Senate seat since the 17th Amendment). Because the party's nominee died, a special GOP primary is scheduled **August 11, 2026**, with a **runoff August 25** if nobody clears 50%. Filing ran July 21-28.

**Context:** Only two people have been elected to this seat since 1956 (Strom Thurmond, Lindsey Graham). Trump carried SC with 58% in 2024. Republicans have won every SC Senate race since 2004.

**Ratings (general):** Cook Solid R | DDHQ Likely R | Inside Elections Likely R | Sabato Safe R.

## Race status

- Regular primary: June 9, 2026
- Special GOP primary: August 11, 2026
- Regular runoff: June 23, 2026
- Special runoff: August 25, 2026 if needed
- General election: November 3, 2026
- Candidate field: 10 active special GOP candidates; SC Votes also showed 2 filed records marked Not Certified for Primary. Confirm later changes against SC Votes.

## Special Republican primary - August 11 (10 active candidates)

1. [[pages/entities/darline-graham|Darline Graham]] - appointed incumbent; sister of Lindsey Graham; ex-SC Commission for the Blind chief
2. [[pages/entities/russell-fry|Russell Fry]] - U.S. Rep, SC-7 (2023-present)
3. [[pages/entities/ralph-norman|Ralph Norman]] - U.S. Rep, SC-5 (2017-present)
4. [[pages/entities/mark-sanford|Mark Sanford]] - former governor (2003-2011), former U.S. Rep
5. [[pages/entities/mark-lynch|Mark Lynch]] - businessman; 28.9% vs Graham in June primary
6. [[pages/entities/mark-mcbride|Mark McBride]] - former Myrtle Beach mayor
7. [[pages/entities/duke-buckner|Duke Buckner]] - attorney; ex-Walterboro councilman
8. [[pages/entities/danny-ford|Danny Ford II]] - lost Ag Commissioner runoff June 23
9. [[pages/entities/glenda-gail-parker|Glenda Gail Parker]] - former Air Force financial manager
10. [[pages/entities/sam-shepherd|Sam Shepherd]] - first-time candidate; little public info

The official SC Votes listing also contains two filed records marked **Not Certified for Primary**: Darius Mitchell and Clark Neilson. They are not included in the active ballot field above.

Open primary: any registered SC voter may vote in it (oath required that you didn't vote in another party's primary that day). Early voting in Anderson County begins Aug 5.
