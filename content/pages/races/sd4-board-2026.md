# Anderson School District 4 Board (2026)

SD4 serves Pendleton (district office at 315 E Queen St, Pendleton, SC 29670). Five board members, 4-year terms, nonpartisan, elected by area at the November general election.

**Seats up Nov 2026 (verified):** Areas 2, 4, and 5, confirmed by the official SC Votes general-election record (election 22596, accessed Aug 17, 2026). This upgrades the earlier inference (Areas 1 and 3 were on the 2024 ballot).

## Race status

- General election: November 3, 2026
- Primary: not applicable, nonpartisan board election
- Runoff: not applicable in the current sources
- Candidate field: **verified from the official SC Votes record**; Area 4 is a three-way contest, Areas 2 and 5 each have one candidate
- Ratings: not applicable
