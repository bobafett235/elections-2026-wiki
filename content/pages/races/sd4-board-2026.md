# Anderson School District 4 Board (2026)

SD4 serves Pendleton (district office at 315 E Queen St, Pendleton, SC 29670). Five board members, 4-year terms, nonpartisan, elected by area at the November general election.

**Seats up Nov 2026 (verified):** Areas 2, 4, and 5, confirmed by the official SC Votes general-election record (election 22596, accessed Aug 17, 2026). This upgrades the earlier inference (Areas 1 and 3 were on the 2024 ballot).

> **November 2 roster check:** the Active candidate field was rechecked against the [official general-election tracker](https://vrems.scvotes.sc.gov/Candidate/CandidateSearch?electionId=22596). This check does not reverify historical results, ratings, biographies, or dated platform comparisons. See [[pages/sources/election-day-2026|Election Day checklist and ballot-coverage limits]].

## Where they disagree (interviews Oct. 26-27; checked Nov. 2, 2026)

The Anderson Observer published direct questionnaires with all three Area 4 candidates. These are attributed candidate positions, not endorsements or independently verified policy outcomes. The August statement that no public platforms were available is superseded by these interviews.

| Issue | Brandon E Bailey | Anna Leavitt | Dustin Williams |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extend the school capital sales tax | Opposes continuation; suggests exploring developer impact fees and a possible hospitality tax | Supports extension to maintain facilities and manage growth without increasing property taxes | Undecided; wants an actual proposal to evaluate and prefers avoiding higher property taxes |
| Partisan school-board elections | No firm preference; emphasizes conservative Christian values and keeping partisan disputes out of classrooms | Strongly opposed; warns of division and dysfunction | Undecided; says military restrictions could prevent him from seeking a partisan seat |
| Growth, facilities, resources | Responsible growth; coordinate infrastructure/public safety; responsible AI and technical-curriculum modernization | Plan for growth while preserving small-community atmosphere; evidence-based resource allocation | Core academics, teacher retention, superintendent accountability; vacant middle-school use and long-term maintenance |

Sources: [Bailey, Oct. 26](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-brandon-e-bailey), [Leavitt, Oct. 27](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-anna-leavitt), [Williams, Oct. 26](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-dustin-williams).

**Do not conflate taxes:** these interview answers concern the school capital sales tax, not necessarily the county capital-projects penny-tax proposal rejected in 2025. A questionnaire about a possible extension is not evidence that a referendum is on the November 2026 ballot. See [[pages/sources/election-day-2026|the official referendum check and sample-ballot guidance]].
