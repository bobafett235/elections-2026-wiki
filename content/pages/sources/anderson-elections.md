# Anderson County Election Sources

## Role

Primary local-government source for Anderson County election administration, county council structure, local elected officials, district information, meeting records, and local election notices.

## Verified scope

- The Registration and Elections office states that it conducts county, state, and federal elections held in Anderson County.
- The county directs users to SC Votes Candidate Tracking for the most current filed-candidate list, so the county page is not itself a complete candidate roster.
- The County Council page describes seven council districts and two-year terms.
- The County Council page provides current district members, biographies, committee assignments, and contact information.

## Integration rules

- Use this source for county structure, incumbency, terms, official biographies, council committees, meeting agendas, and county election notices.
- Use SC Votes for the final candidate and ballot field when the county page points there.
- Do not infer a candidate's reelection status from a current council biography alone.
- Keep district boundaries and Pendleton's District 7 placement linked to the county's district tools or official maps when available.
