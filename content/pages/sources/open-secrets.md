# OpenSecrets

## Role

Secondary, nonprofit research source for federal and state campaign finance, donors, industries, lobbying, outside spending, organizations, and personal-finance records.

## Verified scope

OpenSecrets provides tools for federal members and candidates, state governors and legislators, lobbying, PACs, organizations, industries, outside spending, and donor searches. Its data and analysis are useful for finding patterns that are difficult to see in individual filings.

## Integration rules

- Use official FEC or South Carolina Ethics Commission data first for current-cycle totals.
- Use OpenSecrets for industry and organization classifications, lobbying context, outside spending, and historical comparisons.
- Keep employer-classified individual contributions separate from corporate PAC contributions.
- Record the data vintage and methodology when quoting a sector total.
- Treat written OpenSecrets analysis as reporting or analysis, not as an official finding.

## Relationship to the Influence Registry

The Influence Registry uses OpenSecrets-derived sector data among other inputs. Do not count the two sources as independent corroboration of the same sector figure without checking the underlying source and denominator.

## Current wiki use

Useful for federal pages and the [[pages/sources/influence-registry|Influence Registry]] source note. It is not a substitute for SC Ethics disclosures in state and local races.
