# Ballotpedia

## Role

Secondary election index and structured reference for race pages, candidate discovery, election dates, historical context, and links to local and federal contests.

## Verified scope

The South Carolina 2026 index lists June 9 primaries, June 23 runoffs, and the November 3 general election. It covers U.S. Senate, U.S. House, Governor, statewide offices, State House, special state legislative elections, school boards, and selected local races.

Ballotpedia explicitly states that its candidate list may not include all local candidates and that coverage is subject to its editorial scope.

## Integration rules

- Use Ballotpedia for discovery, race structure, historical results, and a readable overview.
- Cross-check current candidate status against SC Votes or the relevant county election authority.
- Cross-check official results against SC Votes, especially after recounts, court orders, or special elections.
- Keep its race ratings and candidate status dated.
- Do not use it as the only source for a candidate's platform, biography, campaign finance, or ballot status.

## Current wiki use

Ballotpedia remains useful on nearly every race page, but new official source links should take precedence. The Governor page should retain disputed result markers until the official result endpoint is accessible and reconciled.
