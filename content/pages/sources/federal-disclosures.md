# Federal Personal Financial Disclosures

## Role

Primary source for personal financial disclosure reports filed by U.S. House members, Senators, and federal candidates.

## Verified scope

The Senate eFD system provides reports for Senators, former Senators, and Senate candidates filed from 2012 to the present, subject to its retention windows. The House Clerk maintains the House disclosure reports and related public filings.

## Integration rules

- Use these records for disclosed assets, income, liabilities, gifts, transactions, and outside positions.
- Record the report year and report type.
- Keep personal financial disclosure separate from campaign finance and lobbying data.
- Do not turn a disclosed asset, employer, or transaction into an allegation without corroborating reporting or an official finding.
- Prefer the original report PDF or filing record over a summary database.

## Current wiki use

Use selectively on federal candidate and officeholder pages when a financial relationship is relevant to the race. This is not a required field for every candidate.
