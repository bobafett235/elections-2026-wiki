# Candidate Primary Sources

## Role

Official campaign websites, candidate statements, filing documents, questionnaires, debate appearances, and verified campaign accounts provide primary evidence of what a candidate or campaign says.

## Integration rules

- Attribute every platform claim to the campaign, candidate, questionnaire, or event where it appeared.
- Preserve exact issue language when it matters. Do not flatten a broad slogan into a specific policy promise.
- Use official filing records for candidacy, party, office sought, and filing status.
- Use campaign biographies as campaign claims unless independently corroborated by government, institutional, or reputable journalistic sources.
- Do not treat a campaign endorsement list as proof that an organization formally endorsed the candidate without checking the endorsing organization.
- Do not treat social-media posts as stable policy records when a formal campaign page or filing exists.

## Current wiki use

This source class belongs in each candidate page's Platform/Issues section and should be paired with independent reporting or official records for biography, officeholding, and election status.
