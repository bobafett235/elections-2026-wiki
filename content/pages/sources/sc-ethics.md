# South Carolina State Ethics Commission

## Role

Primary source for South Carolina campaign disclosures, candidate Statements of Economic Interest, campaign contribution and expenditure reports, filing deadlines, and disclosure requirements.

## Verified scope

- Candidates must file campaign disclosures after raising or spending $500, including personal funds, campaign loans, and filing fees.
- Candidates with campaign balances file quarterly reports, and candidates must file pre-election disclosures before each election.
- The Commission provides an electronic Public Disclosure and Accountability Reporting System.
- The Commission's campaign pages link the relevant Ethics Reform Act provisions and SC Votes resources.

## Integration rules

- Use this source for Governor, statewide-office, state-legislative, county, and other South Carolina state or local campaign finance.
- Record the report type, covered dates, filing date, and whether the figure is a contribution, expenditure, loan, or balance.
- Do not combine South Carolina disclosure totals with FEC totals without clearly separating jurisdictions and reporting periods.
- Use Statements of Economic Interest for disclosed personal financial interests, not as evidence of corruption or conflicts unless independently established.
- The public-reporting application is dynamic. Preserve the canonical Commission page even when the search application is difficult to fetch automatically.
