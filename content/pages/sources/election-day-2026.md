# Election Day guide – Tuesday, November 3, 2026

Official guidance and candidate records checked November 2, 2026. This is a Pendleton / Anderson County research guide, not a personalized sample ballot.

## Before leaving

1. **Polls are open 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Eastern. Anyone in line at 7:00 p.m. may vote.** ([SEC voter preparation](https://scvotes.gov/voters/2026prep/), [SEC FAQs](https://scvotes.gov/voters/voter-faq/))
2. **Check your assigned polling place**, even if you have voted there before: [Find My Polling Place](https://vrems.scvotes.sc.gov/Voter/Login?PageMode=PollingPlace). Election Day voting is at your precinct’s assigned location, not any early-voting center. Early voting ended October 31; the Pendleton Branch Library’s appearance on an early-voting list does not establish that it is your Election Day polling place. ([SEC FAQs](https://scvotes.gov/voters/voter-faq/), [2026 preparation](https://scvotes.gov/voters/2026prep/))
3. **Open your official [sample ballot](https://vrems.scvotes.sc.gov/Voter/Login?PageMode=Sampleballot).** District boundaries and special districts determine which local contests you receive; a ZIP code alone is not enough. Enter registration information only in the official tool. ([SEC FAQs](https://scvotes.gov/voters/voter-faq/))
4. **Bring a current, valid accepted photo ID:** SC driver’s license; SC DMV ID (including SC concealed weapons permit); SC voter registration card with photo; federal military ID (including qualifying DoD and VA benefits cards); or U.S. passport/passport card. A student ID is not on the SEC’s accepted list. REAL ID is not required: standard SC licenses and ID cards are accepted. ([SEC photo-ID requirements](https://scvotes.gov/voters/photo-id-requirements/))

## If something goes wrong

- **Forgot your photo ID:** you may cast a provisional ballot, but must show the ID to the county elections office before certification for it to count. Ask the office for the exact deadline. ([SEC photo-ID requirements](https://scvotes.gov/voters/photo-id-requirements/))
- **Cannot obtain photo ID because of a reasonable impediment:** bring your current non-photo voter registration card, tell poll managers, and sign the reasonable-impediment affidavit for a provisional ballot. This is a different process from simply forgetting an ID. ([SEC photo-ID requirements](https://scvotes.gov/voters/photo-id-requirements/))
- **Moved recently or registration is unclear:** consult the SEC’s failsafe rules and the county office rather than assuming you cannot vote; eligibility and ballot options depend on when and where you moved. ([SEC FAQs](https://scvotes.gov/voters/voter-faq/))
- **Anderson County Registration & Elections:** **864-260-4035**, **301 North Main Street, Anderson, SC 29621**. The county lists Election Day service hours as **7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.** This office address is not a claim about your assigned polling place. ([County office](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/departments-a-z/vote/))

## Still holding an absentee ballot?

- It must be **received by the county voter registration office by 7:00 p.m. November 3**, not merely postmarked. With Election Day this close, personal delivery avoids relying on mail arrival. Bring accepted photo ID for an in-person return. ([SEC absentee rules](https://scvotes.gov/voters/absentee-voting/))
- Sign the voter’s oath and have the signature witnessed by someone age 18 or older; a notary is not required. Follow both-envelope instructions. ([SEC absentee rules](https://scvotes.gov/voters/absentee-voting/))
- Returning another person’s ballot has separate immediate-family/authorized-representative, authorization-form, photo-ID, and return-limit rules. Read the official instructions or call the office first. Do not assume any friend can deliver it. ([SEC absentee rules](https://scvotes.gov/voters/absentee-voting/))
- If the absentee ballot has not been returned, the SEC says an in-person provisional ballot can count as long as the absentee ballot is not returned before polls close. Ask poll managers for help; do not attempt to vote twice. ([SEC FAQs](https://scvotes.gov/voters/voter-faq/))

### Ballot questions

The official referendum search for election 22596 returned local questions but **no Anderson County question or statewide constitutional amendment** in the November 2 retrieval. That is the tracker’s observed coverage, not proof that an individual ballot has no question. Confirm in the official sample ballot or with the county; do not substitute the wiki’s discussion of the rejected 2025 penny tax for a current ballot check. ([Official referendum tracker](https://vrems.scvotes.sc.gov/Candidate/ReferendumSearch?electionId=22596))

## Election-night results

Results begin appearing **after 7:00 p.m. November 3** through the [official SC Votes results page](https://scvotes.gov/elections-statistics/election-results/). Election-night totals are **unofficial** and can change as remaining ballots are processed. An outlet’s projection is not certification. This pre-election guide records no November winners. ([SEC voter preparation](https://scvotes.gov/voters/2026prep/), [SEC FAQs](https://scvotes.gov/voters/voter-faq/))
