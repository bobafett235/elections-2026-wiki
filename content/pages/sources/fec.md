# Federal Election Commission

## Role

Primary source for federal campaign finance, candidate committees, receipts, disbursements, cash on hand, individual contributions, committee contributions, independent expenditures, and federal filing coverage.

## Verified scope

The FEC provides candidate and committee profiles, election-level summaries, filing records, receipts, disbursements, independent expenditures, and reporting deadlines. Its election pages state that displayed totals may not include the most recently submitted filings.

## Integration rules

- Use the candidate's official FEC profile and filing history for current-cycle figures.
- Record the reporting cutoff or filing date beside every amount.
- Keep direct campaign receipts, party or committee contributions, transfers, independent expenditures, communication costs, and electioneering communications in separate fields where possible.
- Treat FEC-derived totals as federal only. State, county, and school-board races belong with the South Carolina Ethics Commission or the relevant local authority.
- If an election summary says exports are pending or the table is empty, inspect the underlying committee filings before reporting that a candidate raised zero dollars.

## Current wiki use

FEC data should be added first to federal candidate pages and the relevant [[pages/races/senate-2026|Senate]] and House race pages. It should replace career-cumulative finance figures when the question is how much a candidate has raised in the 2026 cycle.
