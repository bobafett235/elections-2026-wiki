# South Carolina and Election News Sources

## Role

Secondary reporting for debates, interviews, withdrawals, court decisions, campaign disputes, local-government context, controversies, and explanations of election events that official records do not explain.

## Priority outlets

- [Anderson Observer](https://www.andersonobserver.com/) for Anderson County government, local development, county council, and Upstate politics.
- [Independent Mail](https://www.independentmail.com/) for Anderson and Upstate election coverage, candidate interviews, and local results context.
- [Greenville News](https://www.greenvilleonline.com/) for statewide and Upstate political reporting.
- [WYFF 4](https://www.wyff4.com/) for broadcast interviews, debates, video, and breaking regional news.
- [Post and Courier Greenville](https://www.postandcourier.com/greenville/) for Upstate political and government reporting.
- [Associated Press Elections](https://apnews.com/hub/elections) for national results, projections, and election reporting.

## Integration rules

- Use a specific article URL, author, and publication date for every claim.
- Use local journalism to explain or corroborate events, not to replace official candidate rosters or certified results.
- Separate reported facts from editorial, opinion, and candidate statements.
- For allegations, preserve the outlet's attribution and seek a primary record or response from the subject.
- Where possible, corroborate a major status change with SC Votes, a court record, or an official government notice.
