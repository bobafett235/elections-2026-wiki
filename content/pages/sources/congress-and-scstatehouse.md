# Congressional and South Carolina Legislative Records

## Role

Primary legislative sources for federal and South Carolina state officeholders, bills, committee assignments, roll-call votes, legislative journals, enacted Acts, and official service history.

## Federal scope

Congress.gov covers the current 119th Congress and historical members, legislation, committees, the Congressional Record, nominations, and roll-call resources. The Senate's South Carolina history page records Lindsey Graham's term ending July 11, 2026, and Darline Graham's appointment beginning July 13, 2026, while Tim Scott remains in the Class 3 seat.

## State scope

South Carolina Legislature Online provides official House and Senate member lists, bill search, tracking, committee postings, journals, roll-call votes, session calendars, Acts, and the South Carolina Code of Laws.

## Integration rules

- Use official member and legislative pages for officeholding, sponsored legislation, committee assignments, and votes.
- Use Congress.gov or the South Carolina Legislature for the underlying record, not a campaign summary.
- Treat Voteview or other ideology tools as derived analysis, not as the voting record.
- Separate an official legislative action from a candidate's campaign position on that action.
- Include session and bill numbers whenever citing a state or federal legislative claim.
