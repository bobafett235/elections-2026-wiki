# Cody Simpson

**Status:** Republican nominee for Commissioner of Agriculture (open seat; Hugh Weathers retiring).

## Background

- Fifth-generation South Carolina farmer ([campaign site](https://www.codyforsc.com/about))
- Former South Carolina State Executive Director of the USDA Farm Service Agency ([campaign site](https://www.codyforsc.com/about))
- Former Agriculture Advisor and Chief Executive Assistant to Governor Henry McMaster ([campaign site](https://www.codyforsc.com/about))
- Graduate degrees in business administration from Francis Marion University ([campaign site](https://www.codyforsc.com/about))
- Campaign states endorsements from President Trump and Gov. McMaster [campaign claim](https://codyforsc.com)
- Won the June 23 GOP runoff (defeated Danny Ford) [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Danny_Ford_(South_Carolina))

## Platform

- "America First Farmer"; protect SC's farms, families, and future [campaign site](https://codyforsc.com)
- Push for stricter inspections to prevent foreign shrimp dumping in the SC market ([Live 5 News](https://www.live5news.com/2026/06/13/we-palmetto-meet-candidate-cody-simpson-ag-commissioner/))
- Protect South Carolina's natural spaces while growing the timber industry responsibly ([Live 5 News](https://www.live5news.com/2026/06/13/we-palmetto-meet-candidate-cody-simpson-ag-commissioner/))
- Ensure the next generation sees agriculture as a promising career path ([MyHorryNews](https://www.myhorrynews.com/opinion/cody-simpson-the-most-important-lessons-are-learned-on-the-farm/article_8fd77adf-f7ef-4683-8845-c2bce0fa1d12.html))
