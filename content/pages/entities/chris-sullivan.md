# Chris Sullivan

**Status:** Republican nominee and the sole active candidate listed by SC Votes for Anderson County Council District 1 in the Nov. 3 general election. He defeated Kelley Koonce in the June 9 Republican primary.

## Background

- Current District 1 council member and local entrepreneur, according to the [Anderson County Council page](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/government/county-council/).
- The county describes Sullivan as a lifelong Anderson County resident and University of Georgia business-administration graduate ([county profile](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/government/county-council/)).

## Platform

- Sullivan told the Independent Mail that he prioritizes quality, high-paying industry while preserving local character, managed growth, and infrastructure ([pre-primary profile](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/03/whos-running-key-issues-in-anderson-county-council-primary/90175653007/)).
