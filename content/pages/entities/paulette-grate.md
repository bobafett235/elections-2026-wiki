# Paulette Grate

**Status:** Incumbent Anderson School District 4 board member, Area 2; running for reelection on Nov 3, 2026. Confirmed as the sole Area 2 candidate by the official SC Votes general-election record (Aug 17, 2026).

## Background

- Assumed office in 2010 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Anderson_School_District_4,_South_Carolina,_elections))
- Serves as vice chairperson of the Anderson School District Four Board of Trustees ([SCSBA](https://scsba.org/paulette-grate/))
- Has 12 years of school board experience ([SCSBA](https://scsba.org/paulette-grate/))
- Earned a nursing degree from Clemson University in 1978 ([SCSBA](https://scsba.org/paulette-grate/))
- Listed in the Anderson County current elected officials record ([county PDF](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Current-Elected-Officials-2024.pdf))
