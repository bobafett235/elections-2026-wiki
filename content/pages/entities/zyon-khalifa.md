# Zyon Khalifa

**Status:** Democratic nominee for U.S. House in South Carolina's 2nd Congressional District; challenges 25-year incumbent Joe Wilson on Nov 3, 2026. Listed as Active on the official SC Votes general-election record (election 22596, accessed Aug 17, 2026).

## Background

- 25 years old; political newcomer who pointed to his age and outsider status as evidence that voters wanted different leadership ([WLTX](https://www.wltx.com/article/news/politics/elections/zyon-khalifa-wins-democratic-runoff-south-carolinas-2nd-congressional-district/101-33ba25d9-1b65-4ad8-8108-5585a9922edf))
- Former University of South Carolina athlete and attorney ([Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/columbia/news/zyon-khalifa-democratic-nomination-2nd-district/article_12ca7365-3022-4b4f-a854-ba1861917faf.html))
- Won the June 23 Democratic runoff over David Robinson II with 53.8% to 46.2% ([Center Square](https://www.thecentersquare.com/south_carolina/article_92c4e9d5-6294-4606-87f9-54a65f6a310a.html))
- Said he "grew up where we're cooking Roger Woods on the stove because we're hungry and that's all we have" ([Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/columbia/news/zyon-khalifa-democratic-nomination-2nd-district/article_12ca7365-3022-4b4f-a854-ba1861917faf.html))
- Completed Ballotpedia's 2026 Candidate Connection survey ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Zyon_Khalifa))

## Platform

- Top priorities: infrastructure, affordability, and opportunity ([Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/columbia/news/zyon-khalifa-democratic-nomination-2nd-district/article_12ca7365-3022-4b4f-a854-ba1861917faf.html))
- Campaign site says the campaign is focused on community growth, job opportunities, and effective leadership ([campaign site](https://www.khalifaforcongress.com/))
- Framed the general election as "a David versus Goliath scenario" ([WLTX](https://www.wltx.com/article/news/politics/elections/zyon-khalifa-wins-democratic-runoff-south-carolinas-2nd-congressional-district/101-33ba25d9-1b65-4ad8-8108-5585a9922edf))
