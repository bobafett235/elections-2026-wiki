# Darline Graham

**Status:** Appointed U.S. Senator (July 13, 2026); won the Aug. 25 special Republican runoff with 193,486 votes (52.45%) over Ralph Norman's 175,412 (47.55%). She is the Republican nominee for the Nov. 3 general election and is marked Active in the official SC Votes tracker.

## Background

- Sister of Lindsey Graham; appointed by Gov. McMaster to replace him after his death July 11, 2026 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Darline_Graham))
- Former head of the South Carolina Commission for the Blind (130 employees, ~$90M budget) ([campaign site](https://darlinegrahamsc.com))
- Leadership roles at SC Vocational Rehabilitation Department and SC Department of Employment and Workforce [campaign site](https://darlinegrahamsc.com)
- Master's in rehabilitation counseling from SC State University [campaign site](https://darlinegrahamsc.com)
- Campaign describes her as the first female senator from South Carolina [campaign claim](https://darlinegrahamsc.com)

## Platform

- Conservative values, "lifetime of service" framing [campaign site](https://darlinegrahamsc.com)
- Helping South Carolinians with disabilities find employment and live independently [campaign site](https://darlinegrahamsc.com)
- Fiscally conservative: "fiercely protective of taxpayer dollars" [campaign site](https://darlinegrahamsc.com)
- Runs on agency-management record: turned around the Commission for the Blind (130 employees, 10 offices, ~$90M budget); workforce-development experience at SC Vocational Rehabilitation and the Department of Employment and Workforce ([campaign](https://darlinegrahamsc.com))
- Emphasizes honoring Lindsey Graham's legacy and her appointment as South Carolina's first female senator ([campaign](https://darlinegrahamsc.com))
- Campaign states she is endorsed by Donald Trump [campaign claim](https://darlinegrahamsc.com)
