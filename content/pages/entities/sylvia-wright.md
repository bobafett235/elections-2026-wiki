# Sylvia Wright

**Status:** Democratic nominee for Superintendent of Education; educator.

## Background

- Career educator with experience as a 6th and 7th grade teacher, instructional coach, and curriculum specialist ([campaign site](https://www.wright2026sc.com/about))
- Holds a Master of Education in Educational Leadership and is pursuing a Doctor of Education at the University of South Carolina ([campaign site](https://www.wright2026sc.com/about))
- Former District Coordinator for Secondary Education in St. George, SC ([campaign site](https://www.wright2026sc.com/about))
- Advanced from the June 9, 2026 Democratic primary ([Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm))

## Platform

- "Restore the Promise of Public Education"; describes SC public education as "in crisis" [campaign site](https://www.wright2026sc.com/about)
- Ensure every 4-year-old in South Carolina has access to a Universal Pre-K program ([The Item](https://www.theitem.com/stories/state-superintendent-of-education-d-lisa-ellis-sylvia-wright,443037))
- Evolve student teaching into a paid internship model ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/sylvia-wright))
- Provide enhanced tax credits and childcare vouchers to educators ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/sylvia-wright))
- Responsibly leverage AI technology and the blended learning model in districts ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/sylvia-wright))
