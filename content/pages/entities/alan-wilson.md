# Alan Wilson

**Status:** Republican nominee for governor; sitting SC Attorney General (2011-present).

## Background

- SC Attorney General since January 12, 2011 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Alan_Wilson_(South_Carolina)))
- Colonel in the South Carolina Army National Guard, nearly 30 years of service; deployed overseas during Operation Iraqi Freedom II; Combat Action Badge ([campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com))
- Graduate of the U.S. Army War College [campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com)
- Led the creation of the South Carolina Human Trafficking Task Force in 2012 ([campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com))
- Former assistant in the 11th Circuit Solicitor's Office and the SC AG's office [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Alan_Wilson_(South_Carolina))
- Son of U.S. Rep. Joe Wilson (SC-2) [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Alan_Wilson_(South_Carolina))
- Won the June 23 GOP runoff; Ballotpedia currently reports 68.4% (211,555 votes) over Lt. Gov. Pamela Evette. The initial wiki draft recorded 68.6% [disputed] ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_gubernatorial_and_lieutenant_gubernatorial_election,_2026))
- Campaign reported endorsements from seven other state attorneys general in March 2026 ([Fox Carolina](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/03/17/seven-attorneys-general-endorse-sc-ag-alan-wilson-governor/))

## Platform

- "Law and order" plan emphasizing public safety and protection of victims ([Fox Carolina](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/03/17/seven-attorneys-general-endorse-sc-ag-alan-wilson-governor/))
- Tax reform, including elimination of the state income tax ([Live 5 News](https://www.live5news.com/2026/06/24/south-carolina-governors-race-set-wilson-johnson-turn-general-election/))
- Government transparency and affordability ([Live 5 News](https://www.live5news.com/2026/06/24/south-carolina-governors-race-set-wilson-johnson-turn-general-election/))
- Improve education, infrastructure, and healthcare access while cutting government spending ([Greenville Online](https://www.greenvilleonline.com/story/news/politics/2026/05/11/alan-wilson-explains-what-sets-him-apart-sc-governors-race/90032053007/))
- Combating child exploitation: cites 1,271 child predator convictions; expanded ICAC Task Force [campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com)
- Aligns with the MAGA agenda; supports policies to protect conservative values [campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com)
- Election integrity [campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com)
