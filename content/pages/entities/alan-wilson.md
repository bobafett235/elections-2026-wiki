# Alan Wilson

**Status:** Republican nominee for governor; sitting SC Attorney General (2011-present).

## Background

- SC Attorney General since January 12, 2011 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Alan_Wilson_(South_Carolina)))
- Colonel in the South Carolina Army National Guard, nearly 30 years of service; deployed overseas during Operation Iraqi Freedom II; Combat Action Badge ([campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com))
- Graduate of the U.S. Army War College [campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com)
- Led the creation of the South Carolina Human Trafficking Task Force in 2012 ([campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com))
- Former assistant in the 11th Circuit Solicitor's Office and the SC AG's office [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Alan_Wilson_(South_Carolina))
- Son of U.S. Rep. Joe Wilson (SC-2) [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Alan_Wilson_(South_Carolina))
- Won the June 23 GOP runoff; official SC Election Commission ENR record (100% counties certified): 218,569 votes (68.56%) over Lt. Gov. Pamela Evette (100,227, 31.44%). This corrects the earlier Ballotpedia-sourced figure of 68.4% / 211,555 votes, which does not match the certified record ([ENR](https://www.enr-scvotes.org/SC/126718/))
- Campaign reported endorsements from seven other state attorneys general in March 2026 ([Fox Carolina](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/03/17/seven-attorneys-general-endorse-sc-ag-alan-wilson-governor/))

## Platform

- "Law and order" plan emphasizing public safety and protection of victims ([Fox Carolina](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/03/17/seven-attorneys-general-endorse-sc-ag-alan-wilson-governor/))
- Tax reform: eliminate the state income tax and reduce property taxes, including fixing Act 388 ([Live 5 News](https://www.live5news.com/2026/06/24/south-carolina-governors-race-set-wilson-johnson-turn-general-election/), [Charleston City Paper](https://charlestoncitypaper.com/2026/07/10/high-prices-take-center-stage-in-s-c-governors-race/))
- Government waste: "Families First Audit Initiative" of independent state-agency audits; cut fraud, waste, and abuse ([iVoterGuide survey](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/24114/race/27677/election/1421))
- Affordability: tort reform to lower insurance costs, expand "affordable, reliable energy production," and fix Act 388 property taxes (campaign statements reported by the [Charleston City Paper](https://charlestoncitypaper.com/2026/07/10/high-prices-take-center-stage-in-s-c-governors-race/))
- Abortion: human life protected from conception; abortion limited to narrow circumstances (life of the mother, rape/incest, serious medical emergency); supports enforcing the Comstock Act; no taxpayer funding for abortion providers ([iVoterGuide survey](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/24114/race/27677/election/1421))
- Minimum wage: wages best set by the market, not government mandates ([iVoterGuide survey](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/24114/race/27677/election/1421))
- Healthcare: maintain Medicaid and Medicare for those who rely on them; no expansion of government-run programs ([iVoterGuide survey](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/24114/race/27677/election/1421))
- Education: eliminate the U.S. Department of Education and return control to states and parents; parents have the fundamental right to direct a child's education and health decisions ([iVoterGuide survey](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/24114/race/27677/election/1421))
- Transgender policy: opposes physical or social transition of children; biological males should not participate in women's sports or occupy women's spaces ([iVoterGuide survey](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/24114/race/27677/election/1421))
- Immigration: opposed to sanctuary cities; law enforcement should cooperate with federal authorities ([iVoterGuide survey](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/24114/race/27677/election/1421))
- Election integrity: supports voter ID laws; says he defended them as attorney general ([iVoterGuide survey](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/24114/race/27677/election/1421))
- Combating child exploitation: cites 1,271 child predator convictions; expanded ICAC Task Force [campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com)
- Energy: diversified portfolio with emphasis on natural gas and nuclear baseload; disputes that man-made climate change requires "urgent political action" ([iVoterGuide survey](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/24114/race/27677/election/1421))
- Second Amendment: "The Second Amendment shall not be infringed"; prosecute criminals who break the law ([iVoterGuide survey](https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/24114/race/27677/election/1421))
- Aligns with the MAGA agenda; supports policies to protect conservative values [campaign site](https://wilsonforsc.com)
