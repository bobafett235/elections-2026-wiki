# Jason Belton

**Status:** Democratic nominee for Secretary of State (won the June 9 primary).

## Background

- Labor organizer and community activist ([Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm))
- Vice President of the Greater Columbia Central Labor Council ([campaign site](https://www.jasonbeltonforsc.com/))
- Former Executive Director of the SC Statehouse Legislative Progressive Caucus ([campaign site](https://www.jasonbeltonforsc.com/blank))
- Crisis Director for the South Carolina National Action Network and executive board member of Justice 360 ([campaign site](https://www.jasonbeltonforsc.com/blank))
- Co-owner and operations manager of the family business Belton Does It Best; financial officer for C&J Consulting LLC ([campaign site](https://www.jasonbeltonforsc.com/blank))

## Platform

- Make the Secretary of State's office support the formation and growth of small businesses and nonprofits in all sectors ([WSOC candidate guide](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/political-beat-candidate-guide-south-carolina-secretary-state/67ZKLKSKQZGEVGCPF5QIOVZPSU/))
- Modernize online services, speed up filings, and cut red tape for small businesses ([WSOC candidate guide](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/political-beat-candidate-guide-south-carolina-secretary-state/67ZKLKSKQZGEVGCPF5QIOVZPSU/))
- Make the office more accessible, simpler to understand, and cheaper to file ([WSOC candidate guide](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/political-beat-candidate-guide-south-carolina-secretary-state/67ZKLKSKQZGEVGCPF5QIOVZPSU/))
