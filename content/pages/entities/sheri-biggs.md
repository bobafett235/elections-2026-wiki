# Sheri Biggs

**Status:** Incumbent U.S. Representative for SC-3 (assumed office Jan 3, 2025); won the June 9, 2026 Republican primary and is listed for the November 3 general election ([FEC](https://www.fec.gov/data/elections/house/SC/03/2026/)).

## Background

- Born March 28, 1970, in Kosciusko, Mississippi; graduated Kosciusko High School in 1988 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheri_Biggs))
- Nurse practitioner; earned a bachelor's degree from Carolina College of Biblical Studies and a Doctor of Nursing Practice from Samford University ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Sheri_Biggs))
- Served in the Mississippi Air National Guard, retiring at the rank of lieutenant colonel ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheri_Biggs))
- Elected in 2024 to the open seat left by retiring Rep. Jeff Duncan; finished second in a seven-way primary and won the June 25 runoff over Mark Burns, a pastor who claimed Trump's endorsement ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/south-carolina-burns-biggs-runoff-sister-senators-330b71548d5e6b5058327055337c8a9b); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheri_Biggs))
- Third woman from South Carolina elected to the U.S. House, and the first to represent SC-3 ([house.gov](https://sheribiggs.house.gov/about))
- Serves on the Homeland Security; Foreign Affairs; and Science, Space, and Technology committees ([house.gov](https://sheribiggs.house.gov/about))
- Member of the House Freedom Caucus ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheri_Biggs))

## Platform

- Campaign profile emphasizes faith, military service, and working with President Trump on the district's pressing issues ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/south-carolina-burns-biggs-runoff-sister-senators-330b71548d5e6b5058327055337c8a9b))
- Introduced the Improving Travel for Military Members Act to create dedicated TSA lanes for active-duty members; her office says the bill passed markup in the House Homeland Security Committee ([sheribiggs.house.gov](https://sheribiggs.house.gov))
- In an Aug 13, 2026 interview, highlighted the Made in America Defense Act and military-family travel legislation, called affordable healthcare a frequent constituent concern, and said she expects the House to pass the Lindsey Graham Russian Sanctions Act ([WYFF](https://www.wyff4.com/article/sheri-biggs-south-carolina-senate-runoff/73430915))
- Supports school choice and parental empowerment ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/sheri-biggs))
