# Mike Burkhold

**Status:** Republican candidate for Comptroller General (open seat; Brian Gaines not running).

## Background

- Charleston-based businessman and founder of Equiscript, a healthcare technology company ([candidate profile](https://samueleburns.substack.com/p/mike-burkhold-a-conservative-candidate))
- Spent fifteen years working with hard numbers in accounting and finance ([campaign site](https://burkhold.net))
- Unsuccessful candidate for South Carolina's 5th congressional district in 1998 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_South_Carolina_State_Comptroller_election); [Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm))

## Platform

- "Count the state's money and show you where it goes" [campaign site](https://burkhold.net)
- Publish the state's annual checkbook online for full transparency of expenditures ([candidate profile](https://samueleburns.substack.com/p/mike-burkhold-a-conservative-candidate))
- Create a Public Corruption Unit to investigate misconduct and fiscal mismanagement ([candidate profile](https://samueleburns.substack.com/p/mike-burkhold-a-conservative-candidate))
- Advocate for counties to take over more local road management to reduce state expenses ([candidate profile](https://samueleburns.substack.com/p/mike-burkhold-a-conservative-candidate))
- Implement a searchable, accessible database to eliminate the need for FOIA requests on spending data ([candidate profile](https://samueleburns.substack.com/p/mike-burkhold-a-conservative-candidate))
- Claims SC's books were off by $3.5 billion [campaign site](https://burkhold.net)
