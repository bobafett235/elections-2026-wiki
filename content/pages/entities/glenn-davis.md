# Glenn Davis

**Status:** Democratic incumbent and active general-election candidate for Anderson County Council District 2. SC Votes lists Republican James Hayes as his Nov. 3 opponent.

## Background

- Current District 2 council member, owner of Davis Driving Academy, and retired Michelin employee ([county profile](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/government/county-council/)).
- The county describes Davis as a lifelong Anderson County resident who attended Tri-County Technical College and Limestone College ([county profile](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/government/county-council/)).

## Platform

- Public safety, managed growth, increased community outreach, and transparency regarding council spending ([Independent Mail](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/03/whos-running-key-issues-in-anderson-county-council-primary/90175653007/))
- Emphasis on restoring parks and providing community activities ([WYFF 4](https://www.wyff4.com/article/anderson-county-council-district-2-candidates-election/41768616))
