# Vincent Coe

**Status:** Democratic nominee for State Treasurer (won the June 9 primary).

## Background

- Bank officer and former bank examiner ([Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm))
- Served as a bank examiner for both the FDIC and the Federal Reserve, with over 20 years protecting financial systems ([campaign site](https://vincentforsc.com/))
- Experience auditing financial institutions and exposing weak internal controls ([campaign site](https://vincentforsc.com/))

## Platform

- Modernize financial tools and improve internal controls to increase transparency ([WSOC candidate guide](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/political-beat-candidate-guide-south-carolina-state-treasurer/LYIWGCQOARDQFA6A6YPYO2EVT4/))
- Strengthen accountability by working collaboratively with state agencies and lawmakers ([WSOC candidate guide](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/political-beat-candidate-guide-south-carolina-state-treasurer/LYIWGCQOARDQFA6A6YPYO2EVT4/))
- Protect taxpayer money to prevent delays or mismanagement of refunds ([WISTV](https://www.wistv.com/2026/05/19/candidates-sc-treasurer-call-transparency-after-inspector-general-report/))
