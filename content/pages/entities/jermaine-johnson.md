# Jermaine Johnson

**Status:** Democratic nominee for governor; SC state representative, HD-52 (since Nov 2024).

## Background

- SC House of Representatives, District 52, since November 11, 2024 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Jermaine_Johnson))
- Defeated a 22-year incumbent in his House primary ([campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com))
- Former professional basketball player ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jermaine_Johnson_(politician)))
- Bachelor's in communication from the College of Charleston (2008); master's in project management from Strayer University (2014); Doctor of Business Administration from Northcentral University (2018) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Jermaine_Johnson))
- Nonprofit CEO/founder ([Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm))
- Campaign describes overcoming homelessness as a youth; describes himself as a "born fighter" [campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com)
- Won the June 9 Dem primary with 59.67% (221,951 votes) over Billy Webster (29.7%) and William Mullins McLeod Jr. (10.7%), per the official SC Election Commission ENR record. This corrects the earlier Ballotpedia-sourced figure of 221,751 votes ([ENR](https://www.enr-scvotes.org/SC/126294/))

## Platform

- Taxes: increase the share of families paying no state income tax from 40% to 70%, arguing full repeal primarily benefits the wealthy; reduce property taxes for working families in partnership with municipalities ([campaign](https://johnsonforsc.com/policies))
- Minimum wage: establish a livable minimum wage through a labor advisory council; remove employment barriers and fight corporate wage theft ([campaign](https://johnsonforsc.com/policies), [Charleston City Paper](https://charlestoncitypaper.com/2026/07/10/high-prices-take-center-stage-in-s-c-governors-race/))
- Education: conduct a full audit of the public education system to increase access and quality; supports trade schools and tech academies modeled on the Lower Richland Tech Academy ([campaign](https://johnsonforsc.com/policies))
- Healthcare: fund the opening of more rural hospitals; protect women's reproductive rights with medical decisions left to families and doctors ([campaign](https://johnsonforsc.com/policies))
- Public safety: raise law enforcement salaries, hire more officers, and add mental-health response teams for crisis de-escalation; jobs program for former felons ([campaign](https://johnsonforsc.com/policies), [ABC News 4](https://abcnews4.com/newsletter-daily/proposed-law-could-transform-jobs-for-630000-former-felons-in-south-carolina-convicted-charged-felonies-donald-trump-scdc-inmate-prison-rehabilitation-second-chance-jobs-money))
- Housing: fund a grant program letting municipalities convert existing structures into affordable housing to compete with private-equity buyers ([campaign](https://johnsonforsc.com/policies), [BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/jermaine-johnson-581d75e6-b26b-4e23-ab5b-ab34ef2500c2))
- Infrastructure: allow municipalities to fix their own roads and bridges with state DOT funds; a bill to require at least 25% in-state production of food, energy, and water ([campaign](https://johnsonforsc.com/policies))
- Cannabis: legalize medicinal marijuana, apply a statewide cannabis tax, commute nonviolent marijuana sentences, and put recreational cannabis to a statewide referendum ([campaign](https://johnsonforsc.com/policies))
- Families: mental-health training for school and community leaders; paid family leave for fathers and a Governor's Commission on Fathers and Families; a hate-crimes law for SC ([campaign](https://johnsonforsc.com/policies))
- Accountability, integrity, commitment to residents [campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com)
- Fund critical state programs that are underfunded [campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com)
- End "single party rule" in SC [campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com)
- Proposes a grant program for municipalities to convert existing structures into affordable housing to compete with private equity firms ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/jermaine-johnson-581d75e6-b26b-4e23-ab5b-ab34ef2500c2))
- Advocates a labor advisory council to recommend a state minimum wage and fight corporate wage theft ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/jermaine-johnson-581d75e6-b26b-4e23-ab5b-ab34ef2500c2))
- Seeks to reduce property taxes for working families ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/jermaine-johnson-581d75e6-b26b-4e23-ab5b-ab34ef2500c2))
- Supports legislation on infrastructure investment, conservation, and criminal justice reform ([PSA Newsroom](https://psanewsroom.com/meet-sc-house-rep-j/))
