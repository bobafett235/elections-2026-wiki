# Jermaine Johnson

**Status:** Democratic nominee for governor; SC state representative, HD-52 (since Nov 2024).

## Background

- SC House of Representatives, District 52, since November 2024 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_South_Carolina_gubernatorial_election))
- Defeated a 22-year incumbent in his House primary ([campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com))
- Nonprofit CEO/founder ([Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm))
- Campaign describes overcoming homelessness as a youth; describes himself as a "born fighter" [campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com)
- Won the June 9 Dem primary with 59.7% (221,751 votes) over Billy Webster (29.7%) and William Mullins McLeod Jr. (10.7%). The initial wiki draft recorded 221,951 votes [disputed] ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_gubernatorial_and_lieutenant_gubernatorial_election,_2026))

## Platform

- Accountability, integrity, commitment to residents [campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com)
- Fund critical state programs that are underfunded [campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com)
- Laws to transform jobs and opportunities for former felons [campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com)
- End "single party rule" in SC [campaign site](https://johnsonforsc.com)
