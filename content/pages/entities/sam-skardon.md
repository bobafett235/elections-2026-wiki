# Sam Skardon

**Status:** Democratic nominee for Lieutenant Governor (running with Jermaine Johnson).

## Background

- Running mate of Jermaine Johnson per the 2026 ticket ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_South_Carolina_gubernatorial_election))
- Former congressional aide to the late U.S. Rep. John Lewis ([SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/07/30/former-aide-to-john-lewis-completes-democratic-ticket-for-sc-governor/))
- Works for the nonprofit Clean Energy Fund of the Carolinas, financing clean energy and EV infrastructure ([SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/07/30/former-aide-to-john-lewis-completes-democratic-ticket-for-sc-governor/))
- Field organizer for the 2008 Obama campaign in South Carolina and Georgia ([WYFF](https://www.wyff4.com/article/jermaine-johnson-sam-skardon-sc-governor-race/73307618))

## Platform

- Build an economy that works for small business owners and working families rather than those with deep pockets ([WYFF](https://www.wyff4.com/article/jermaine-johnson-sam-skardon-sc-governor-race/73307618))
- Increase capital access so more people can build wealth within the state economy ([WYFF](https://www.wyff4.com/article/jermaine-johnson-sam-skardon-sc-governor-race/73307618))
- Joint ticket focus on affordability, public education, and healthcare access ([WYFF](https://www.wyff4.com/article/jermaine-johnson-sam-skardon-sc-governor-race/73307618))
