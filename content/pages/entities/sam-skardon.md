# Sam Skardon

**Status:** Democratic nominee for Lieutenant Governor, running with [[pages/entities/jermaine-johnson|Jermaine Johnson]] on the Nov 3, 2026 general ticket ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_South_Carolina_gubernatorial_election); [SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/07/30/former-aide-to-john-lewis-completes-democratic-ticket-for-sc-governor/)).

## Background

- 38 years old; James Island resident; former chairman of the Charleston County Democratic Party; ran for state Senate in 2020 ([Charleston City Paper](https://charlestoncitypaper.com/2026/07/30/johnson-picks-james-islands-skardon-as-lieutenant-governor-running-mate/))
- Former congressional aide to the late U.S. Rep. John Lewis; joined the staff shortly after the Affordable Care Act became law and spent much of his time helping defend it; as the youngest staff member he often met with students and visitors and heard Lewis tell the civil rights movement story more than a thousand times over four years ([WYFF](https://www.wyff4.com/article/jermaine-johnson-sam-skardon-sc-governor-race/73307618))
- Field organizer for the 2008 Obama campaign, first in South Carolina's presidential primary and later in Georgia's general election ([WYFF](https://www.wyff4.com/article/jermaine-johnson-sam-skardon-sc-governor-race/73307618))
- Father spent 30 years running the Palmetto Project, the statewide nonprofit that introduced the "I Voted" sticker to South Carolina in the 1990s and is the largest enroller of South Carolinians in HealthCare.gov coverage ([WYFF](https://www.wyff4.com/article/jermaine-johnson-sam-skardon-sc-governor-race/73307618))
- Family military tradition: two grandfathers served in World War II and a great uncle survived the Bataan Death March before teaching for decades at Clemson University ([WYFF](https://www.wyff4.com/article/jermaine-johnson-sam-skardon-sc-governor-race/73307618))
- Works for the nonprofit Clean Energy Fund of the Carolinas, financing clean energy and EV infrastructure; Johnson said Skardon has spent the last seven years putting capital into small business and clean energy projects ([SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/07/30/former-aide-to-john-lewis-completes-democratic-ticket-for-sc-governor/); [ABC News 4](https://abcnews4.com/news/local/rep-jermaine-johnson-to-name-lieutenant-governor-pick-as-sc-governor-race-heats-up-local-politics-attorney-general-alan-wilson-november-general-election))

## Platform

- Stated three priorities in Spartanburg: making South Carolina more affordable for working families, improving public education, and investing in infrastructure ([WSPA](https://www.wspa.com/news/sam-skardon-joins-johnson-ticket/))
- "The Wilson family has been in office for 58 years, so this is truly a choice for a new South Carolina and an old South Carolina" ([WSPA](https://www.wspa.com/news/sam-skardon-joins-johnson-ticket/))
- Wants to build an economy that works for small business owners and working families rather than "those with the deepest pockets or the closest ties to Washington and Columbia" ([ABC News 4](https://abcnews4.com/news/local/rep-jermaine-johnson-to-name-lieutenant-governor-pick-as-sc-governor-race-heats-up-local-politics-attorney-general-alan-wilson-november-general-election))
- Joint ticket focus on affordability, public education, and healthcare access ([WYFF](https://www.wyff4.com/article/jermaine-johnson-sam-skardon-sc-governor-race/73307618))
- Says he wants to improve public schools and ensure insurance companies actually cover the health, home, and car costs people pay for ([Charleston City Paper](https://charlestoncitypaper.com/2026/07/30/johnson-picks-james-islands-skardon-as-lieutenant-governor-running-mate/))
