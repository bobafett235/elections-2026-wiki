# Danny Ford

**Status:** Appeared on the Aug. 11 U.S. Senate special Republican primary ballot after a court order required his reinstatement; did not advance to the Aug. 25 runoff.

## Background

- Ran for SC Commissioner of Agriculture in 2026, lost the GOP runoff June 23 to Cody Simpson ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Danny_Ford_(South_Carolina)))
- Filed for the Senate special primary; the state party initially did not certify him, but a court order required his name to be added to all feasible early-voting and Election Day ballots ([SC Votes](https://scvotes.gov/candidate-name-added-to-ballot-in-u-s-senate-special-republican-primary/); [The Sumter Item](https://www.theitem.com/stories/candidate-added-to-ballot-in-us-senate-special-primary,445711))
