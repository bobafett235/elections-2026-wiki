# M. Cindy Wilson

**Status:** District 7 council incumbent through the end of her current term. SC Votes lists Wilson as defeated in the June 9 Republican primary; Collin Alexander won 1,818 votes (50.53%) to Wilson's 1,780 (49.47%), according to the Independent Mail.

## Background

- Real estate broker and farmer ([county profile](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/government/county-council/)).
- Anderson County native, T. L. Hanna High School graduate, and University of South Carolina BFA recipient ([county profile](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/government/county-council/)).
- Member of Concord Baptist Church, Western Upstate Association of Realtors, Anderson County Republican Party, and Republican Women associations ([county profile](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/government/county-council/)).

## 2026 campaign

- Before the primary, Wilson told the Independent Mail that rapid growth and more resident participation in planning and development issues were central concerns ([pre-primary profile](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/03/whos-running-key-issues-in-anderson-county-council-primary/90175653007/)). This is a campaign statement, not a description of official policy.
