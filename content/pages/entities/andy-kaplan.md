# Andy Kaplan

**Status:** Forward Party candidate for U.S. House in South Carolina's 5th Congressional District on the Nov 3, 2026 general ballot. Listed as Active on the official SC Votes general-election record (election 22596, accessed Aug 17, 2026), facing Republican [[pages/entities/wes-climer|Wes Climer]] and Democrat [[pages/entities/mallory-dittmer|Mallory Dittmer]].

## Background

- Healthcare industry professional with more than 30 years of experience and a longtime community leader, having served with organizations including the YMCA, United Way, and local youth programs ([Forward Party](https://www.forwardparty.com/media/blog/forward-party-endorses-first-slate-of-congressional-candidates-for-2026/))
- Says he chose South Carolina 15 years ago; wife Patty has been a public school teacher for 27 years; three kids and a new grandchild ([campaign site](https://www.andykaplan4congress.com/))
- Endorsed by the Forward Party as one of its first slate of congressional candidates for 2026 ([Forward Party](https://www.forwardparty.com/media/blog/forward-party-endorses-first-slate-of-congressional-candidates-for-2026/))
- Ran as an independent under the Forward Party banner rather than through a major party ([campaign site](https://www.andykaplan4congress.com/); [WBTV](https://www.wbtv.com/2026/06/08/tracking-2026-primary-results-south-carolinas-5th-congressional-district-us-house/))

## Platform

- Says money has too much influence and the two-party system keeps the country divided; pledges to "change the rules in Washington" that let special interests control the system ([campaign site](https://www.andykaplan4congress.com/))
- Pillars include practical policies that lower costs, strengthen the economy, and expand opportunity ([campaign site](https://www.andykaplan4congress.com/))
- Campaign frames the goal as cleaning up government and making Congress work for working families ([campaign site](https://www.andykaplan4congress.com/))
