# Annie Andrews

**Status:** Democratic nominee for U.S. Senate; the only major-party general-election alternative to the GOP pick.

## Background

- Won the June 9 Dem primary with 61.5% (225,924 votes) over Brandon Brown (30.2%) and Kyle Freeman (8.3%) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Annie_Andrews))
- Pediatrician with nearly twenty years of experience caring for children in South Carolina ([campaign site](https://drannieandrews.com))
- Continues to practice medicine at a children's hospital while campaigning ([The 19th](https://www.19thnews.org/2026/02/annie-andrews-south-carolina-measles-pediatrician-senate/))
- Ran for U.S. House SC-1 in 2022 (lost to Nancy Mace) [Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm)
- Gun control activist; pediatrician advocate on firearm injury prevention [Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm)
- Has raised over $10 million for her 2026 Senate campaign per recent FEC filings ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Annie_Andrews))

## Platform

- Healthcare as a right, with emphasis on maternal and child health ([campaign site](https://drannieandrews.com))
- Expand Medicaid and increase food assistance for children ([Emily's List](https://emilyslist.org/candidate/annie-andrews/))
- Campaigned heavily on addressing the measles outbreak in South Carolina as a public health priority ([The 19th](https://www.19thnews.org/2026/02/annie-andrews-south-carolina-measles-pediatrician-senate/))
- Supports reproductive freedom and trusting women's healthcare decisions ([campaign site](https://drannieandrews.com))
- Calls for an end to gun violence to protect children and families ([campaign site](https://drannieandrews.com))
