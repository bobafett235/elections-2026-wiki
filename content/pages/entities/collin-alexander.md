# Collin Alexander

**Status:** Republican nominee and the sole active candidate listed by SC Votes for Anderson County Council District 7 in the Nov. 3 general election. He defeated incumbent M. Cindy Wilson in the June 9 Republican primary, 1,818 votes (50.53%) to 1,780 (49.47%), according to the Independent Mail.

## Background

- The State Election Commission's candidate record documents Alexander's active general-election filing for District 7 ([candidate detail](https://vrems.scvotes.sc.gov/Candidate/CandidateDetail/?candidateId=23680&electionId=22596&searchType=Default)).
- Alexander told the Independent Mail that he is a Citadel graduate and retired U.S. Marine Corps member ([pre-primary profile](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/03/whos-running-key-issues-in-anderson-county-council-primary/90175653007/)).
- In his April announcement, Alexander said he returned to Anderson County after military service and works in technology ([The Journal Online](https://thejournalonline.com/2026/04/30/collin-alexander-announces-for-county-council-7-seat/)).

## Platform

- Alexander's campaign said it supported tighter FILOT agreement review, a third-party county-finance audit, stricter conflict-of-interest rules, meeting streaming, transparency, growth limits, and road funding tied to major-corporation agreements ([campaign statements reported by the Independent Mail](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/03/whos-running-key-issues-in-anderson-county-council-primary/90175653007/) and [The Journal Online](https://thejournalonline.com/2026/04/30/collin-alexander-announces-for-county-council-7-seat/)).
