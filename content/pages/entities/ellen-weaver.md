# Ellen Weaver

**Status:** Incumbent Superintendent of Education (since January 11, 2023), running for re-election.

## Background

- Assumed office January 11, 2023; elected as the 19th State Superintendent of Education in November 2022 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Ellen_Weaver); [ed.sc.gov](https://ed.sc.gov/about/superintendent-of-education/))
- Former president and CEO of the Palmetto Promise Institute, a conservative think tank ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Ellen_Weaver))
- Former leader of a think tank supporting public funding of private schools ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellen_Weaver))
- Former state director with the U.S. Senate [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Ellen_Weaver)
- Board member of the SC Governor's School for the Arts and Humanities [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Ellen_Weaver)
- B.A. from Bob Jones University (2001) [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Ellen_Weaver)

## Platform

- Expand education choice and parental empowerment ([campaign site](https://ellenweaver.com/))
- Implement bold literacy and math reforms in South Carolina classrooms ([campaign site](https://ellenweaver.com/))
- Reduce red tape for teachers so they can focus on teaching ([campaign site](https://ellenweaver.com/))
- Campaign profile includes rejecting "woke ideology" in the state's educational curriculum ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellen_Weaver))
