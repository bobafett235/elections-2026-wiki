# Don Chapman

**Status:** Incumbent SC House member, District 8; won the June 23 GOP runoff (vs Sherry Hodges) and faces Demetrius Carson in November.

## Background

- First elected to the South Carolina House in 2022 ([Anderson Observer](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/incumbent-rep-chapman-wins-reelection-to-sc-house-dist-8-seat))
- Born in Yokosuka, Japan; lives in Townville, SC ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Don_Chapman))
- B.S. interior design and M.Arch from SCAD (1989) [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026)
- President and architect, Chapman Design Group; master builder, Icon Construction of SC [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026)
- Former president of the Home Builders Association of Anderson; former board member, Anderson Area Chamber of Commerce [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026)
- Won the June 23 primary runoff over Sherry Hodges (Patrick Orr eliminated June 9) ([Independent Mail](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/23/sc-election-runoff-results-hodges-vs-chapman-anderson-county-district-8/90648743007/); [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026))

## Platform

- Describes himself as a "glass-half-full type of guy" who avoids negative campaigning ([Independent Mail](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/23/sc-election-runoff-results-hodges-vs-chapman-anderson-county-district-8/90648743007/))
- Improving roads, reducing congestion and potholes in Anderson County ([campaign](https://chapmanforschouse.com))
- Supports raising teacher pay and expanding school choice ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/don-chapman))
- Pro-life; supports constitutional rights, stopping crime, and border security ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/don-chapman))
