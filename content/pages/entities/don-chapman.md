# Don Chapman

**Status:** Incumbent SC House member, District 8; survived a June GOP primary runoff (vs Sherry Hodges).

## Background

- Born in Yokosuka, Japan; lives in Townville, SC ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Don_Chapman); election context from [South Carolina House elections](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026))
- B.S. interior design and M.Arch from SCAD (1989) [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026)
- President and architect, Chapman Design Group; master builder, Icon Construction of SC [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026)
- Former president of the Home Builders Association of Anderson [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026)
- Former board member, Anderson Area Chamber of Commerce [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026)
- Won the June 23 primary runoff over Sherry Hodges (Patrick Orr eliminated June 9) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Carolina_House_of_Representatives_elections,_2026))
