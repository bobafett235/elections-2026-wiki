# Kasie Whitener

**Status:** Libertarian nominee for U.S. Senate; won the party convention April 11, 2026 over Jason Brenkus.

## Background

- Clinical Assistant Professor of entrepreneurship/strategic management at USC Darla Moore School of Business; director of the Faber Entrepreneurship Center [Candidate Connection survey via ballotpedia.org/Kasie_Whitener]
- Radio host: co-hosts "Mornings with Kasie and Kev" on 100.7 The Point; hosts "Write On SC" [Candidate Connection]
- Author of two novels: After December (2019) and Before Pittsburgh (2021) [Candidate Connection]
- B.A. and M.A. in English from Clemson and Winthrop [Candidate Connection]
- Chair of the SC Libertarian Party state committee; former Richland County chair [Candidate Connection]

## Platform

- Opposes the "corrupt duopoly" of the two-party system ([campaign site](https://kasiesouthcarolina.com))
- Balanced budget amendment; reducing the national debt [campaign site](https://kasiesouthcarolina.com)
- End protectionist policies and laws [campaign site](https://kasiesouthcarolina.com)
- Real oversight of government; anti-corruption [campaign site](https://kasiesouthcarolina.com)
- Active Senate oversight role; fiscal responsibility, 5% per-department spending cuts per year until debt is under control [Candidate Connection]
