# Kasie Whitener

**Status:** Libertarian nominee for U.S. Senate; won the party convention April 11, 2026 over Jason Brenkus.

## Background

- Teaching Associate Professor at the Darla Moore School of Business at the University of South Carolina; director of the Faber Entrepreneurship Center ([LP.org](https://lp.org/candidate/kasie-whitener/); [Candidate Connection via Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Kasie_Whitener))
- Ph.D. in Organization and Management (2014) ([LP.org](https://lp.org/candidate/kasie-whitener/))
- Author of novels After December (2019) and Before Pittsburgh (2021) ([LP.org](https://lp.org/candidate/kasie-whitener/))
- B.A. and M.A. in English from Clemson and Winthrop [Candidate Connection]
- Chair of the SC Libertarian Party state committee; former Richland County chair [Candidate Connection]
- Radio host: co-hosts "Mornings with Kasie and Kev" on 100.7 The Point; hosts "Write On SC" [Candidate Connection]

## Platform

- Opposes the "corrupt duopoly" of the two-party system ([campaign site](https://kasiesouthcarolina.com))
- Balanced budget amendment; reducing the national debt [campaign site](https://kasiesouthcarolina.com)
- End protectionist policies and laws to allow greater economic prosperity [campaign site](https://kasiesouthcarolina.com)
- Real government oversight; reducing government interference in personal and business affairs ([campaign site](https://kasiesouthcarolina.com))
- Positions herself as independent and accessible representation for constituents over party operatives ([campaign site](https://kasiesouthcarolina.com))
- Fiscal responsibility; 5% per-department spending cuts per year until debt is under control [Candidate Connection]
