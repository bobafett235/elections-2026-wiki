# Mark Hammond

**Status:** Incumbent Secretary of State (since 2003), running for re-election.

## Background

- Secretary of State of South Carolina since 2003 ([Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm))
- Former criminal investigator for the 7th Circuit Solicitor's Office ([sos.sc.gov](https://sos.sc.gov/about-us/secretarys-biography))
- Former juvenile probation officer with the South Carolina Department of Youth Services ([Vote Smart](https://justfacts.votesmart.org/candidate/biography/47881/mark-hammond))
- Completed special basic training at the South Carolina Criminal Justice Academy in 1991 ([sos.sc.gov](https://sos.sc.gov/about-us/secretarys-biography))
- Implemented "Business One Stop" for business filing efficiency ([campaign site](https://hammond4sc.com))

## Platform

- Target charity and telemarketing abuses through regulatory reforms ([sos.sc.gov](https://sos.sc.gov/about-us/secretarys-biography))
- Increase accountability and transparency for nonprofits through improvements to the Solicitation of Charitable Funds Act ([sos.sc.gov](https://sos.sc.gov/about-us/secretarys-biography))
- Focus on efficiency, accountability, and customer service within the Secretary of State's office ([sos.sc.gov](https://sos.sc.gov/about-us/secretarys-biography))
- High Growth Small Business Job Creation Act: increase investment in early-stage businesses [campaign site](https://hammond4sc.com)
- Highlights SC's top-5 national ranking for charitable giving under his oversight [campaign site](https://hammond4sc.com)
