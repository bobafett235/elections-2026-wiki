# Jimmy Davis

**Status:** Republican incumbent and the sole active candidate listed by SC Votes for Anderson County Council District 6 in the Nov. 3 general election. He defeated Ryan Cowsert in the June 9 primary.

## Background

- Current District 6 council member and small-business principal ([county profile](https://www.andersoncountysc.org/government/county-council/)).
- His campaign says he was first elected in 2018 and serves on the county Planning and Public Works Committee while chairing the Personnel Committee ([campaign site](https://www.votejimmydavis.com/)).

## Platform

- Davis's campaign emphasizes taxpayer protection, public safety, community outreach and transparency in council spending, and infrastructure before construction permitting ([campaign site](https://www.votejimmydavis.com/)).
