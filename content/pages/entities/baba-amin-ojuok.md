# Baba Amin Ojuok

**Status:** United Citizens Party candidate for Superintendent of Education on the Nov 3, 2026 general ballot. Listed as Active on the official SC Votes general-election record (election 22596, accessed Aug 17, 2026), joining Republican [[pages/entities/ellen-weaver|Ellen Weaver]] and Democrat [[pages/entities/sylvia-wright|Sylvia Wright]].

## Background

- Founder of The Uhuru Academy, an African-centered online K-12 private school with students across the country; co-founder and CEO of Uhuru Academy Education Solutions, LLC, providing educational solutions since 2010 ([The State via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/sc-superintendent-ellen-weaver-3-121753676.html); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/babaamin/))
- Hopkins, SC resident who moved to South Carolina from Texas in 2019 ([The State via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/sc-superintendent-ellen-weaver-3-121753676.html))
- Reports 30-plus years working with youth and families ([The State via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/sc-superintendent-ellen-weaver-3-121753676.html))
- Had not filed a campaign finance report as of mid-2026 ([The State](https://www.thestate.com/news/politics-government/article315434210.html))
