# Mike Reichenbach

**Status:** Republican nominee for Lieutenant Governor (running with Alan Wilson).

## Background

- SC state senator, District 31 (Darlington and Florence Counties), since 2022 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_South_Carolina_gubernatorial_election))
- Businessman and owner of several Ford car dealerships in the Florence area ([Post and Courier](https://www.postandcourier.com/politics/south-carolina-governor-wilson-reichenbach-2026/article_794c5456-8abe-4fec-bdd3-7c0e29097ded.html))
- First Black man to serve in the SC Senate as a Republican ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Reichenbach_(politician)))
- Tapped as Wilson's running mate January 2026 [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_South_Carolina_gubernatorial_election)

## Platform

- Use his background in finance and business to improve government performance and efficiency ([WPDE](https://wpde.com/news/local/wilson-highlights-running-mate-government-audit-proposal))
- Protect families and values while making the state more affordable for families and profitable for businesses ([The State](https://www.thestate.com/news/politics-government/article314260835.html))
- Support a government accountability initiative to increase efficiency in state operations ([WPDE](https://wpde.com/news/local/wilson-highlights-running-mate-government-audit-proposal))
