# Glenda Gail Parker

**Status:** Defeated in the August 11, 2026 special Republican primary; did not advance to the August 25 runoff.

## Background

- Former financial manager for U.S. Air Force Research and Development operations ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Glenda_Gail_Parker))
- Retired Major in the U.S. Air Force Reserves ([Fox Carolina](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/08/04/us-senate-special-election-candidate-profile-glenda-gail-parker/))
- Retired Pentagon budget analyst with 36 years of service as a civil servant ([campaign site](https://glendagailparker.com/))
- Experience as an elections official ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Glenda_Gail_Parker))
- Previously ran for Virginia House of Delegates (2019) and U.S. House VA-1 (2014, 2016), plus twice for U.S. Senate in Virginia ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Glenda_Gail_Parker); [Fox Carolina](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/08/04/us-senate-special-election-candidate-profile-glenda-gail-parker/))
- Graduate degree in business administration; software engineering administration certificate [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Glenda_Gail_Parker)

## Platform

- Transportation investment, including high-speed rail along the I-95 South corridor ([Fox Carolina](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2026/08/04/us-senate-special-election-candidate-profile-glenda-gail-parker/))
- Accountability, transparency, and citizen-focused leadership ([campaign site](https://glendagailparker.com/))
- Improved care for veterans through the VA ([WMBF News](https://www.wmbfnews.com/2026/08/08/where-sc-senate-special-election-candidates-stand-issues/))
- Past themes: building all types of rail (high speed, light rail, passenger, freight); auditable accounting at the Pentagon; eliminating the Foreign Military Sales program; opposition to no-bid contract awards ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Glenda_Gail_Parker))
