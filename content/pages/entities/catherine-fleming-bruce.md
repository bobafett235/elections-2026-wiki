# Catherine Fleming Bruce

**Status:** Democratic write-in candidate for U.S. Senate in the November 3, 2026 general election, per the current Ballotpedia candidate listing.

## Background

- Author, preservationist, and activist from South Carolina ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Fleming_Bruce))
- Earned a degree from Agnes Scott College and a master's degree in mass communication and information studies from the University of South Carolina ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Catherine_Fleming_Bruce))
- Served as a foreperson for a South Carolina State Grand Jury ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Catherine_Fleming_Bruce))
- Ran unsuccessfully for South Carolina Democratic Party chair in 2023 ([The State](https://www.thestate.com/news/politics-government/article312879293.html))
- Previously ran for U.S. Senate in 2022, losing in the primary runoff ([The State](https://www.thestate.com/news/politics-government/article312879293.html))

## Platform

- Campaign frames the race around justice, a high quality of life, and liberty ([ABC News 4](https://abcnews4.com/news/local/catherine-fleming-bruce-announces-run-for-lindsey-grahams-senate-seat-election-2026-politics))
- Public profile emphasizes culture and social justice ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Fleming_Bruce))
