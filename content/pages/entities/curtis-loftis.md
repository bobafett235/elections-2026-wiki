# Curtis Loftis

**Status:** Incumbent State Treasurer (since 2011), running for re-election.

## Background

- State Treasurer since 2011 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Curtis_Loftis,_Jr.))
- Manages and invests over $76 billion in public funds as the state's banker ([treasurer.sc.gov](https://treasurer.sc.gov/about-us/meet-the-treasurer/))
- Chairman of the State Board of Financial Institutions; vice chairman of the State Fiscal Accountability Authority ([treasurer.sc.gov](https://treasurer.sc.gov/about-us/meet-the-treasurer/))
- Founded Home Pest Control Company in 1981, managed until 2001; owner of several central South Carolina businesses ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Curtis_Loftis,_Jr.))
- Founder and principal benefactor of the Saluda Charitable Foundation ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Curtis_Loftis,_Jr.); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_Loftis))
- Former state chairman of Mitt Romney's 2012 campaign in South Carolina ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_Loftis))

## Platform

- Maintain strong credit ratings for South Carolina to save taxpayers money ([treasurer.sc.gov](https://treasurer.sc.gov/about-us/newsroom/statement-from-south-carolina-state-treasurer-curtis-loftis-on-the-office-of-inspector-general-report/))
- Expand personal finance education through the SC Financial Literacy Master Teacher Program ([treasurer.sc.gov](https://treasurer.sc.gov/))
- Improve the performance and funding of the South Carolina Retirement System ([treasurer.sc.gov](https://treasurer.sc.gov/about-us/newsroom/leadership-corner-south-carolina-treasurer-curtis-loftis/))
