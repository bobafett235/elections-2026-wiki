# Ralph Norman

**Status:** U.S. Representative for SC-5 (2017-present); advanced to the Aug. 25 special GOP runoff against Darline Graham after taking 82,627 votes (24.62%) in the Aug. 11 special primary. Certified totals from the official State Election Commission ENR record.

## Background

- U.S. House SC-5 since 2017 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Norman))
- SC House of Representatives 2005-2007 and 2009-2017 [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Norman)
- Real estate developer, Warren Norman Company [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Norman)
- Ranked most conservative member of the 117th Congress by GovTrack (Feb 2023) [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Norman)
- Ran for governor in 2026 before pivoting to the Senate race; eliminated in the June 9 gubernatorial primary, endorsed Wilson ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Norman))
- NOT a candidate for his SC-5 House seat in the November general election: the official SC Votes general-election record (Aug 17, 2026) shows no Norman filing for SC-5; the seat's field is Wes Climer (R), Mallory Dittmer (D), and Andy Kaplan (Forward). Norman is running only for Senate.
