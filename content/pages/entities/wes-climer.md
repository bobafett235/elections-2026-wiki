# Wes Climer

**Status:** Republican nominee for U.S. House in South Carolina's 5th Congressional District, the open seat left by [[pages/entities/ralph-norman|Ralph Norman]]. Listed as Active on the official SC Votes general-election record (election 22596, accessed Aug 17, 2026), facing Democrat [[pages/entities/mallory-dittmer|Mallory Dittmer]] and Forward Party candidate [[pages/entities/andy-kaplan|Andy Kaplan]].

## Background

- Born September 26, 1982 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Climer))
- South Carolina Senate District 15 (York County) member since 2016, when he defeated incumbent Robert W. Hayes Jr. in the Republican primary; endorsed by then-Gov. Nikki Haley and supported by the Club for Growth in that race ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Climer))
- Financial adviser and longtime state legislator ([Almanac of American Politics via Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Wes_Climer))
- Announced his congressional campaign July 31, 2025, in Rock Hill ([Rock Hill Herald](https://www.heraldonline.com/news/politics-government/article311540366.html))
- Ralph Norman endorsed Climer to succeed him in Congress ([WIS, Aug 21, 2025, via Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Climer))
- Faced no opponent in the June 9 Republican primary ([Rock Hill Herald](https://www.heraldonline.com/news/politics-government/article315896792.html))
- Submitted an "irrevocable resignation" from the state Senate effective Nov 3, 2026, triggering a special election for SD-15 on the same day as the general election ([SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/06/22/gop-senator-running-for-congress-resigns-enabling-voters-to-replace-him-in-november/))

## Platform

- Campaign priorities announced at launch: cutting the federal budget, sealing the Southern border, fighting Critical Race Theory in schools, and overhauling the Department of Justice; also better federal/state/local coordination on growth ([Rock Hill Herald](https://www.heraldonline.com/news/politics-government/article311540366.html))
- Says his record in the state Senate is pro-growth: cutting red tape, lowering costs, and helping businesses create jobs ([U.S. Chamber of Commerce](https://www.uschamber.com/improving-government/u-s-chamber-endorses-candidate-wes-climer-for-south-carolinas-fifth-congressional-district))
- Endorsed by the U.S. Chamber of Commerce ([U.S. Chamber of Commerce](https://www.uschamber.com/improving-government/u-s-chamber-endorses-candidate-wes-climer-for-south-carolinas-fifth-congressional-district))
