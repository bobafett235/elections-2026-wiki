# Brandon E Bailey

**Status:** Candidate for Anderson School District 4 board, Area 4, on Nov 3, 2026. Listed as Active on the official SC Votes general-election record (election 22596, checked Nov. 2, 2026); Area 4 is a three-way race with [[pages/entities/anna-leavitt|Anna Leavitt]] and [[pages/entities/dustin-williams|Dustin Williams]].

## Background

- Official tracker ballot name is “Brandon E Bailey”; the earlier filing notice rendered the surname “Baily”. The official spelling is retained. ([Official tracker](https://vrems.scvotes.sc.gov/Candidate/CandidateSearch?electionId=22596))

The following biographical statements are **candidate self-reports** in the Anderson Observer’s 2026-10-26 questionnaire, read Nov. 2. They are not independently credential-verified.

- Says he taught college for ten years, works in technology, and manages multimillion-dollar budgets and projects. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-brandon-e-bailey))
- Says his children are the fourth generation in Anderson 4 schools; reports certifications in Google Workspace for Education and Microsoft for Education. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-brandon-e-bailey))

## Platform

- **Opposes continuing the school capital improvements sales tax**, citing household tax and cost burdens. Proposes exploring developer impact fees and says he could support a hospitality sales tax for schools. These are proposals, not verified legal authority or adopted policy. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-brandon-e-bailey))
- Emphasizes responsible growth, infrastructure/public-safety coordination, school safety, teacher support, and fiscal transparency. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-brandon-e-bailey))
- Calls for responsible, secure AI use, attention to AI-assisted cheating versus learning, and updated technical curricula/equipment. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-brandon-e-bailey))
- Does not take a firm position for or against partisan school-board elections; describes himself as a Christian with conservative values and says many partisan disputes do not belong in classrooms. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-brandon-e-bailey))
