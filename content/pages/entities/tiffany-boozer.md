# Tiffany Boozer

**Status:** Democratic nominee for Comptroller General (open seat; Brian Gaines not running).

## Background

- Quality assurance manager at the Comptroller's Office; accountant ([Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm))
- Certified Public Accountant with over 10 years of audit experience in government and private sectors ([The Item](https://www.theitem.com/stories/comptroller-general-d-tiffany-boozer-cpa-bruce-k-cole-phd-cpa,443048))
- Current internal auditor within the South Carolina Comptroller General's Office ([The Item](https://www.theitem.com/stories/comptroller-general-d-tiffany-boozer-cpa-bruce-k-cole-phd-cpa,443048))
- Treasurer and graduate of the Talented Tenth Organization ([The Item](https://www.theitem.com/stories/comptroller-general-d-tiffany-boozer-cpa-bruce-k-cole-phd-cpa,443048))
- Founder of Blacklist Network, a professional networking organization ([The Item](https://www.theitem.com/stories/comptroller-general-d-tiffany-boozer-cpa-bruce-k-cole-phd-cpa,443048))

## Platform

- Ensure the state's accounting office is led by a trained accountant for accurate and transparent financial reporting ([campaign site](https://boozerforsc.com/))
- Strengthen internal controls and oversight to reduce errors and improve reporting consistency ([The Item](https://www.theitem.com/stories/comptroller-general-d-tiffany-boozer-cpa-bruce-k-cole-phd-cpa,443048))
- Modernize outdated state financial processes to improve efficiency and timeliness ([The Item](https://www.theitem.com/stories/comptroller-general-d-tiffany-boozer-cpa-bruce-k-cole-phd-cpa,443048))
- Enhance the transparency portal with year-over-year comparisons of public spending trends ([The Item](https://www.theitem.com/stories/comptroller-general-d-tiffany-boozer-cpa-bruce-k-cole-phd-cpa,443048))
