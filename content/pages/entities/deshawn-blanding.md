# DeShawn Blanding

**Status:** Democratic candidate for Commissioner of Agriculture (open seat; Hugh Weathers retiring).

## Background

- Fourth-generation farmer from the rural Silver community in Clarendon County ([CAES News](https://caes.news/south-carolina-agriculture-commissioner-blanding/); [campaign site](https://blandingforsc.com))
- Former policy analyst for the U.S. House Committee on Agriculture ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_South_Carolina_Commissioner_of_Agriculture_election))
- Former policy and strategy specialist for the Rural Coalition and senior Washington representative for the Food and Environment Program ([CAES News](https://caes.news/south-carolina-agriculture-commissioner-blanding/))
- First HBCU-educated national officer of FFA ([CAES News](https://caes.news/south-carolina-agriculture-commissioner-blanding/))
- Agricultural co-op president; former congressional aide ([Politics1](https://politics1.com/sc.htm))

## Platform

- "Honoring what built us while building what comes next"; prioritizes "Processing, Partnerships, and Protection" in agriculture [campaign site](https://blandingforsc.com)
- Establish a Regional Agricultural Infrastructure and Innovation Network and community food hubs ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/deshawn-blanding))
- Prioritize food and market access, local processing, and economic development ([CAES News](https://caes.news/south-carolina-agriculture-commissioner-blanding/))
- Expand technical training, farm apprenticeships, and support for FFA programs ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/deshawn-blanding))
- Utilize a cooperative business model for agriculture to prioritize local job development ([CAES News](https://caes.news/south-carolina-agriculture-commissioner-blanding/))
