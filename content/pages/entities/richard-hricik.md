# Richard Hricik

**Status:** Democratic nominee for Attorney General.

## Background

- Owner and trial attorney, Law Offices of Richard A. Hricik, P.A. ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Richard_Hricik))
- Former public accountant at Arthur Andersen; former faculty at Charleston Southern University [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Richard_Hricik)
- Worked blue-collar jobs: forklift operator, painter, auto mechanic [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Richard_Hricik)
- Ran for SC Senate District 43 in 2020 [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Richard_Hricik)
- J.D. from William & Mary Law School (1993) [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Richard_Hricik)
- Former congressional aide per Politics1 [politics1.com/sc.htm]

## Platform

- No donations from PACs, corporations, or special interests ([campaign site](https://hricikforattorneygeneral.com))
- Protect families, individual rights, consumer money, natural resources [campaign site](https://hricikforattorneygeneral.com)
- Modernize the AG's office with data and analytics [campaign site](https://hricikforattorneygeneral.com)
- Combat corporate fraud, consumer price gouging, environmental polluters (e.g., plastic nurdles) [campaign site](https://hricikforattorneygeneral.com)
- Hold government officials accountable for corruption and waste [campaign site](https://hricikforattorneygeneral.com)
