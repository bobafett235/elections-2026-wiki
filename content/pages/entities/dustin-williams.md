# Dustin Williams

**Status:** Candidate for Anderson School District 4 board, Area 4, on Nov 3, 2026. Listed as Active on the official SC Votes general-election record (election 22596, checked Nov. 2, 2026); Area 4 is a three-way race with [[pages/entities/brandon-e-bailey|Brandon E Bailey]] and [[pages/entities/anna-leavitt|Anna Leavitt]].

## Background

The following biographical statements are **candidate self-reports** in the Anderson Observer’s 2026-10-26 questionnaire, read Nov. 2. They are not independently credential-verified.

- Says he graduated from Pendleton High School in 2004, Tri-County Technical College, and Southern Wesleyan; reports more than two decades of military service, including Operation Iraqi Freedom. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-dustin-williams))
- Says his wife teaches and his children attend district schools; reports volunteering with the local fire department and coaching YMCA soccer. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-dustin-williams))

## Platform

- Prioritizes core academic outcomes, parental rights, teacher recruitment/retention, financial stewardship, and superintendent accountability. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-dustin-williams))
- Is **undecided** on extending the school capital sales tax. Says he wants to examine an actual proposal before deciding and prefers avoiding higher property taxes. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-dustin-williams))
- Identifies future use of the vacant middle-school property and long-term facilities maintenance as challenges. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-dustin-williams))
- Is **undecided** on partisan school-board elections; says military-service restrictions could prevent him from seeking a partisan seat. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-dustin-williams))
