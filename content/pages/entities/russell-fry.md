# Russell Fry

**Status:** U.S. Representative for SC-7 (2023-present); did not advance from the Aug. 11 special GOP primary. His November House candidacy still needs a direct SC Votes filing or sample-ballot check.

## Background

- U.S. House SC-7 since 2023 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Fry))
- SC House of Representatives 2015-2023, including Majority Chief Whip [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Fry)
- J.D. from Charleston School of Law [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Fry)
- Attorney before elected office [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Fry)
