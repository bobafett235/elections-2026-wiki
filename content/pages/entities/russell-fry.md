# Russell Fry

**Status:** U.S. Representative for SC-7 (2023-present); finished third in the Aug. 11 special GOP primary (65,955 votes, 19.66%) and did not advance. Confirmed by the official SC Votes general-election record (Aug 17, 2026) as the Active Republican candidate for SC-7 on the Nov. 3 ballot.

## Background

- U.S. House SC-7 since 2023 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Fry))
- SC House of Representatives 2015-2023, including Majority Chief Whip [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Fry)
- J.D. from Charleston School of Law [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Fry)
- Attorney before elected office [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Fry)

## Platform

- "America First": secure borders, lower costs, energy dominance ([campaign site](https://russellfrysc.com))
- Strong support for Trump's judicial nominees [campaign site](https://russellfrysc.com)
- Finish the border wall, end "catch and release" [campaign site](https://russellfrysc.com)
- Combat the fentanyl crisis; punish traffickers [campaign site](https://russellfrysc.com)
