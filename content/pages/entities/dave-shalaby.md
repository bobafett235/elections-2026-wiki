# Dave Shalaby

**Status:** Republican nominee and the sole active candidate listed by SC Votes for Anderson County Council District 4 in the Nov. 3 general election. He won the open-seat Republican primary over Samuel Whatley.

## Background

- President of Poly-Med, a biotechnology company in Anderson, according to the Independent Mail's pre-primary profile ([source](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/03/whos-running-key-issues-in-anderson-county-council-primary/90175653007/)).
- District 4 was open after Vice Chairman Brett Sanders did not seek reelection ([Independent Mail result report](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/09/results-anderson-county-council-2026-sc-primary-election/90402445007/)).

## Platform

- Shalaby told the Independent Mail he favored a third-party county-finance audit, road repair and maintenance, and tighter conflict-of-interest rules for council and Planning Commission work ([pre-primary profile](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/03/whos-running-key-issues-in-anderson-county-council-primary/90175653007/)).
