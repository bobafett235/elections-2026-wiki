# Mark Sanford

**Status:** Former governor and congressman; running in the Aug 11 special GOP primary. Declined the governor race and the SC-1 House race to run here.

## Background

- Governor of South Carolina 2003-2011 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Mark_Sanford_(South_Carolina)))
- U.S. House SC-1: 1995-2001 and 2013-2019 [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Mark_Sanford_(South_Carolina))
- U.S. Air Force Reserve 2002-2011 [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Mark_Sanford_(South_Carolina))
- Ran for president in 2020 [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Mark_Sanford_(South_Carolina))
- Fled to Argentina in 2009 while governor ("Appalachian Trail" scandal), finished term as a lame duck [general-knowledge; well documented]
- Most recently a CNN contributor and author [general-knowledge; verify before relying on]

## Platform

- Primary focus: national debt and deficit spending ([campaign site](https://marksanford.com))
- Argues federal debt will crush the economy and destroy the American Dream [campaign site](https://marksanford.com)
- Financial discipline; return to the Founding Fathers' beliefs on taxation [campaign site](https://marksanford.com)
