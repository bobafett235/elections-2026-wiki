# Mark Sanford

**Status:** Conceded the August 11, 2026 special GOP Senate primary; did not advance to the August 25 runoff.

## Background

- Governor of South Carolina 2003-2011 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Mark_Sanford_(South_Carolina)))
- U.S. House SC-1: 1995-2001 and 2013-2019 [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Mark_Sanford_(South_Carolina))
- U.S. Air Force Reserve 2002-2011 [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Mark_Sanford_(South_Carolina))
- Ran for president in 2020 [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Mark_Sanford_(South_Carolina))
- Known as a staunch fiscal conservative who frequently clashed with Donald Trump over government spending ([NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/11/us/politics/mark-sanford-south-carolina-senate-candidate.html))
- Fled to Argentina in 2009 while governor ("Appalachian Trail" scandal), finished term as a lame duck [general-knowledge; well documented]
- Declined the governor race and the SC-1 House race to run in the Senate special primary; conceded Aug 11 ([Fox Carolina](https://www.foxcarolina.com/video/2026/08/12/mark-sanford-concedes-special-gop-primary-us-senate/); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/11/politics/south-carolina-republican-senate-special-primary))

## Platform

- Centered the campaign on the "financial tipping point" of the United States ([campaign site](https://marksanford.com))
- Advocates drastic reductions in federal deficit spending to prevent inflation and protect the American dream ([campaign site](https://marksanford.com))
- Argues high federal debt acts as a deferred tax on future generations ([campaign site](https://marksanford.com))
- Financial discipline; return to the Founding Fathers' beliefs on taxation [campaign site](https://marksanford.com)
