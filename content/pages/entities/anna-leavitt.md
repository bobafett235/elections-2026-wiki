# Anna Leavitt

**Status:** Candidate for Anderson School District 4 board, Area 4, on Nov 3, 2026. Listed as Active on the official SC Votes general-election record (election 22596, checked Nov. 2, 2026); Area 4 is a three-way race with [[pages/entities/brandon-e-bailey|Brandon E Bailey]] and [[pages/entities/dustin-williams|Dustin Williams]].

## Background

The following biographical statements are **candidate self-reports** in the Anderson Observer’s 2026-10-27 questionnaire, read Nov. 2. They are not independently credential-verified.

- Describes herself as a retired pediatrician with 30 years of practice and a 30-year member of the community; says her two children attended Anderson 4 schools. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-anna-leavitt))
- Says her medical experience taught careful listening, evidence-based decisions, questioning, and collaboration. This is the local candidate’s account, not a biography inferred from a same-name person elsewhere. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-anna-leavitt))

## Platform

- **Supports extending the school capital sales tax**, arguing it maintains facilities and accommodates growth without increasing property taxes. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-anna-leavitt))
- **Strongly opposes partisan school-board elections**, saying they risk division and dysfunction without benefiting students. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-anna-leavitt))
- Prioritizes planning for growth while preserving the district’s small-community atmosphere, maintaining educational standards, and allocating limited resources responsibly. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-anna-leavitt))
- Emphasizes public schools as a shared community asset, board accountability, clear communication, evidence/expert advice, and respect for majority decisions. ([Interview](https://www.andersonobserver.com/news/school-dist-4-area-4-candidate-anna-leavitt))
