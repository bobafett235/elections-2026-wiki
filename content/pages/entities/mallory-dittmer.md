# Mallory Dittmer

**Status:** Democratic nominee for U.S. House in South Carolina's 5th Congressional District. Listed as Active on the official SC Votes general-election record (election 22596, accessed Aug 17, 2026), facing Republican [[pages/entities/wes-climer|Wes Climer]] and Forward Party candidate [[pages/entities/andy-kaplan|Andy Kaplan]].

## Background

- York County business owner and community advocate ([WRHI](https://www.wrhi.com/2025/10/learn-more-about-dittmer-at-https-www-dittmerforcongress-com-205287))
- Of Fort Mill ([SC Daily Gazette via News From The States](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/gop-senator-running-congress-resigns-enabling-voters-replace-him-november))
- Launched her campaign in October 2025 pledging "a new chapter of unity, liberty, and prosperity" for the district ([WRHI](https://www.wrhi.com/2025/10/learn-more-about-dittmer-at-https-www-dittmerforcongress-com-205287))
- Defeated Andrew Clough with 56% of the vote in the June 9 Democratic primary ([SC Daily Gazette via News From The States](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/gop-senator-running-congress-resigns-enabling-voters-replace-him-november))
- Argues the idea that the 5th District is unwinnable for Democrats is "the myth of the fifth" ([Rock Hill Herald](https://www.heraldonline.com/news/politics-government/article315896792.html))

## Platform

- Campaign site says the platform collects "reasonable priorities and practical solutions" gathered from months of traveling the district ([campaign site](https://www.dittmerforcongress.com/))
- Lower child care costs and improve access, citing one of the highest child care cost-to-income ratios in the US ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/mallory-dittmer))
- Uphold the Constitution, checks and balances, and congressional authority, and hold authoritarian tactics and rhetoric to account ([BallotReady](https://www.ballotready.org/people/mallory-dittmer))
