# Josh Mann

**Status:** Republican nominee and the sole active candidate listed by SC Votes for Anderson County Council District 5 in the Nov. 3 general election. He defeated incumbent chairman Tommy Dunn in the June 9 primary.

## Background

- Campaign describes Mann as a veteran, former Anderson City firefighter, local small-business owner, and Gospel Light Church member ([campaign site](https://www.votejoshmann.com/)).
- The Independent Mail reported that Mann defeated Dunn in the District 5 Republican primary ([result report](https://www.independentmail.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/06/09/results-anderson-county-council-2026-sc-primary-election/90402445007/)).

## Platform

- Mann's campaign emphasizes transparency in county spending, fiscal accountability, resident input, and growth aligned with infrastructure ([campaign site](https://www.votejoshmann.com/)).
