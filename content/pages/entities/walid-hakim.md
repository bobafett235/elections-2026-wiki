# Walid Hakim

**Status:** Green Party candidate for governor.

## Background

- Born to an American mother and a Greek/Palestinian father ([campaign site](https://walidnhakim.com))
- Enlisted in the U.S. Marine Corps in 1990 after the invasion of Kuwait [campaign site](https://walidnhakim.com)
- Varied career: police officer, political consultant, actor, car salesman, restaurant owner [campaign site](https://walidnhakim.com)
- Attended Lexington High School and Newberry College [campaign site](https://walidnhakim.com)
- Gun rights activist and USMC veteran per Politics1 [politics1.com/sc.htm]

## Platform

- "100 Year Plan" for a brighter future [campaign site](https://walidnhakim.com)
- Education reform: "Education For All" [campaign site](https://walidnhakim.com)
- Law and order reform: "Protection Under The Law" [campaign site](https://walidnhakim.com)
- Land reform for improved communities [campaign site](https://walidnhakim.com)
- Grassroots democracy, social justice, environmental sustainability [campaign site](https://walidnhakim.com)
